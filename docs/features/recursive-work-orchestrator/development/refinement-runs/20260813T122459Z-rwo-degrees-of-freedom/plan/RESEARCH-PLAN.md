# Non-Executed Research Plan: System Degrees of Freedom

Status: candidate plan; not executed  
Research owner: unresolved; recommended repository-wide authority/capability research with RWO and
ACI as primary consumers  
Research mode: local evidence first, then one separately approved bounded external comparison

## Objective

Recommend a closed V1 capability envelope and an evidence-gated later target without confusing
expressivity, authority, capability grants, enforcement, or receipts.

## Phase 1 — Freeze definitions and universe

Outputs:

- glossary, including the five meanings of “orchestrable”;
- closed actor, phase, transition-class, Freedom Slice, path-kind, and receipt-type catalogs;
- ATS schema and coverage-ledger schema;
- exact current/V1/later status axes;
- frozen utility and attack scenario cards.

Gate: every seed freedom maps into the closed universe; every omission is explicit.

## Phase 2 — Reconstruct current state

Use accepted decisions, code and executed tests, and as-built operational evidence. For every ATS:

- identify the authenticated or claimed initiator;
- reconstruct grant ancestry and authority owner;
- identify actual enforcement paths and bypasses;
- inventory observed receipt types and claim ceilings;
- classify current behavior without filling unknowns from proposals.

Gate: all load-bearing current claims cite code/test/as-built evidence. Requested constraints are not
reported as fences.

## Phase 3 — Bounded external precedent pass

Requires a new operator confirmation. Freeze these comparison questions first:

1. How do object-capability systems model attenuation, delegation, revocation, audience, and
   non-transferability?
2. How do workflow/scheduler systems distinguish nested composition from nested scheduler
   authority and conserve global budgets?
3. How do policy decision/enforcement architectures bind decisions to physical enforcement and
   durable denial evidence?
4. How do sandbox/tool mediation systems prove filesystem, process, network, secret, and egress
   confinement?
5. How are effect receipts, unknown outcomes, idempotency, reconciliation, and compensation kept
   distinct from authorization and delivery?

Corpus policy: primary standards, official system documentation, and research papers only. External
owners supply precedent labels and failure modes; they do not select CyberAlchemy's decision.

Gate: every external finding answers one frozen question and is labeled `build-from-owned`,
`already-deployed`, or `precedent-clean`; novelty is not the goal.

## Phase 4 — Candidate V1 envelope

Populate ATS records and project them into the matrix. Produce:

- `allowed`, `approval-gated`, `unavailable`, or `out-of-model` availability;
- `delegable-attenuated`, `root-only`, `forbidden-to-delegate`, or `n/a` delegability;
- separate current behavior, claim basis, recommended V1 and later-target fields;
- specific restriction relaxation ladders.

Candidate starting point to test:

- broad bounded composition under one root scheduler/authority root;
- task-specific leaf grants with no implicit inheritance;
- confined local mutation and bounded subprocesses when their fences are proven;
- adapter-mediated external effects only under exact approval and effect reconciliation;
- authority-root creation, capability self-issuance, policy/tool/adapter mutation and automatic
  dynamic-graph promotion unavailable or root-only in V1.

Gate: candidate is a recommendation, not an accepted decision.

## Phase 5 — Falsification

Run all scenario cards as ATS traces. Check:

- qualitative attenuation;
- quantitative conservation at checkpoints and globally;
- no scheduler-equivalent escape;
- no context/data-to-authority promotion;
- distinct typed receipts and honest unknowns;
- exact reconfirmation triggers;
- five utility witnesses remain possible;
- twelve attack families fail closed or expose a named model gap.

Gate: a confirmed amplification path blocks the proposed envelope until repaired. An unavailable
current fence does not kill the desired V1 behavior; it labels it unavailable today.

## Phase 6 — Decision packet

Return:

1. one-page executive recommendation;
2. glossary and semantic split;
3. current-state enforcement map;
4. ATS dataset and coverage ledger;
5. current/V1/later capability matrix;
6. utility and attack results;
7. typed authority and receipt chain;
8. relaxation evidence ladders;
9. decisions requiring human/owner judgment;
10. rejected alternatives and residue.

Required owner decisions:

- Is one authority root per confirmed run the V1 rule?
- Is nested authority permanently forbidden or merely unavailable in V1?
- Which actions are root-only versus separately approval-gated?
- Which local-mutation route, if any, meets the V1 confinement standard?
- Which effect adapters are eligible for V1 and under what evidence?
- Who owns the canonical capability envelope and its evolution?

## Suggested independent roles

- repository precedent mapper;
- runtime/enforcement investigator;
- object-capability/delegation researcher;
- workflow/scheduler-composition researcher;
- sandbox/tool/effect-boundary researcher;
- authority-amplification skeptic;
- V1 utility auditor;
- synthesizer and independent final auditor.

Exploration roles should remain independent until their evidence packets are frozen. The skeptic
tests named gates; the synthesizer cannot erase dissent; the final auditor checks every V1 claim
against evidence and scenarios.

## Write and authority boundaries

Research writes only under its own research folder. It does not mutate RWO, ACI, runtime, policy,
capability, UI, or decision artifacts. Acceptance and implementation require separate owner routes.

## Completion evidence

- closed catalogs and schemas;
- source manifest or pinned selectors;
- cited ATS/current-state dataset;
- complete coverage ledger;
- executed scenario matrix with reproducible traces;
- external-source decision and, if approved, cited comparison artifact;
- independent skeptical and final-audit receipts;
- explicit owner decision packet.
