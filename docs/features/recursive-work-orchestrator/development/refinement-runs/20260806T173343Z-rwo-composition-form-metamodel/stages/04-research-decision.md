## Research lifecycle

Three explorers covered formal form semantics, adversarial condition/owner boundaries, and ontology/schema/validation architecture. The synthesizer selected a common-envelope candidate, but the reviewers found three material failures:

1. existing form, selector, release, lifecycle, graph, and owner types were being duplicated;
2. generic condition/evaluator and external-reference surfaces could become a universal policy engine;
3. a scenario checklist was being presented too close to a witness package despite having no serialized fixtures or executed validation.

The single allowed feedback loop repaired the candidate by deleting those surfaces, reusing current owners, splitting compilation success/rejection and defect ownership, and narrowing the evidence ceiling.

## Exact research decision

Proceed with `CompositionFormSourceContract@candidate-2` only as:

- an immutable authored instance of one existing `CompositionForm` subtype;
- closed subtype-specific fields and existing selector/mapping/policy relations;
- deterministic source-to-`WorkGraph` compilation with disjoint results;
- compiler-only structural defects;
- typed, non-authorizing references to external owner contracts;
- validation-first planned fixture contracts.

Kill parallel form/condition/evaluator/reference ontologies and any runtime-shaped form instance.

## Evidence closure

It does not override any reviewer block for adoption, migration, implementation, conformance, or promotion.
