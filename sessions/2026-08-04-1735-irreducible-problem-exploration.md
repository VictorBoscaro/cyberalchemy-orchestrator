# Repository Irreducible Problem Exploration

## Summary

The repository objective is to keep agent work connected to the objectives, decisions, assumptions, actions, and evidence that give it meaning while improving collective judgment. This session revised the [research brief](../research/repository-irreducible-problem-inventory/research-initial-definitions.md) so it asks a neutral, falsifiable question and preserves source standing. It designed a staged investigation across objective and judgment, authority and composition, and execution and realization, with mandatory sources plus justified additional reading. Two launch attempts failed closed because of invalid generated task names, a missing automatic host hook, altered manually relayed envelopes, and reserved retry names. The successful retry used unique task names, programmatically extracted launch arguments, direct bridge binding, and pre-launch verification of all three seats. Three explorers completed source-grounded reports and all bindings reached `resolved`. Their evidence rejects a premature single-problem thesis and instead identifies candidate families around purpose continuity, collective judgment, semantic integrity, authority and licensed use, and realization with corrective closure. The session also confirmed a documented contradiction between the claimed mandatory Codex hook path and the observed ability to spawn without `PreToolUse`. The exploration was persisted in [research.md](../research/repository-irreducible-problem-inventory/stages/exploration/research.md) and distilled in [findings.md](../research/repository-irreducible-problem-inventory/stages/exploration/findings.md), without promoting a complete or irreducible final inventory.
