# The T0-root spine: UNITY → P-FACES-INSTANCE, FRACTALITY → HYP-ORCH-FRACTAL, and the close-enrich audit

## Summary

The session began as a top-down orientation to the repo and became locating and re-homing its
conceptual spine. Reading `vault/axioms.md`, we found AX-2 already frames the scientific process as
the T0 root with decision-science, categorical, and engineering as three faces = instances of it,
and AX-3 admits self-similarity/fractality "remains to be earned" — i.e. the epistemic and
operational vocabularies are not orthogonal but faces of one root. A tensioned pair (constructive
category-theory vs Popperian skepticism) decided how to give that structural claim a falsifiable
home. Decision: split the UNITY claim (faces are instances) into a named premise **P-FACES-INSTANCE**
under a trimmed AX-2, and the FRACTALITY claim (ascension-loop ≡ orchestration-loop across scales)
into a new **private** hypothesis **HYP-ORCH-FRACTAL** gated below OBL-E3; the non-standard `private`
key was registered in `ontology-conventions` (v1.1.0). A tensioned review caught and fixed a
re-immunization (the OBL-E3 gate had become an OQ-9 retreat clause), pinned "≡" to full+faithful, and
applied the OQ-11 fix to P-FACES-INSTANCE's decision-moving falsifier. The ledger audit then **fired**
falsifier 1 — provable from the appender's strict schema, no close-row can enrich `C` — so
HYP-ORCH-FRACTAL is **double-gated on BL-3** (a typed-graph ledger). Freezing P-FACES-INSTANCE's
correspondence map showed the engineering face's `enrich` operation has no correspondent at current
design (the same joint, a partial fire); the owner deferred binding the decision-moving disjunct. Two
dispatches were self-registered and closed in the ledger (A6); a concurrent process moved
`vault/axioms.md` into `vault/axioms/` and restructured it (AX-4/AX-5), absorbing this session's
content intact, and the move-broken links were fixed.

enrichment). The landed audit [[close-row-enrich-c]] — provable from the appender schema, not sampled —
licenses this: it is the single double-gate on both P-FACES-INSTANCE's engineering face and
[[framework-self-similarity]]. The next session should scope BL-3, since nothing on the fractality axis
is runnable until a close can enrich `C`.
