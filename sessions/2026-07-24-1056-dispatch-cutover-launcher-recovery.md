## Summary

opening/close audit ledger during the migration. SQLite owns accepted workflow and provenance facts
for runtime-managed work, while the validated appender remains the sole physical YAML writer.

reconciliation, preservation, rollback, and permission-denial evidence.

- a C0-C3 dispatch/audit-ledger cutover contract with 16 additional cutover and retirement tests;

  scope, restart reconciliation, and 32 acceptance probes;

  unit tests, and an operator runbook.

production cutover remain blocked. No subagent or root action appended to
