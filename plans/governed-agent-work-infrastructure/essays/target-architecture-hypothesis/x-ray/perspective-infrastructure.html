Architecture x-ray / infrastructure truth

One governed path from intent to durable fact.

The hypothesis proposes eight services, separated by responsibility but joined by one invariant: agent work is not trustworthy unless control, content, and evidence remain attributable. This is a proposed finished shape, not a current deployment.

Flow contracts: control, content, and evidence

Control

The plan is checked against the human-confirmed digest before firing. Extra steps fail closed.

Content

Fabric may hold a sealed judgment; it may not rewrite, summarize, choose recipients, or rank.

Evidence

Every act yields an event with axes, binding, and lineage. Durable facts enter only through Record.
