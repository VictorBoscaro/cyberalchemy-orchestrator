One question in. One connected record out.

This is the simplest useful reading of the target architecture hypothesis: decide a repeatable shape of work, let a human confirm that exact shape, run it without changing messages or exposing independent judgments early, and preserve what happened as one traceable record.

Fabric is not a step.

It sits between every pair of agents during the work. It transports and never authors.

Confirmation guards sameness.

The machine plan must match the digest of the shape the human confirmed, or fail closed.

Record is the one writer.

Durable facts enter through Record and nowhere else; corrections append rather than overwrite.

A traceable example

Follow one question through the system

Illustrative route

Example question: “Should we build the proposed target architecture?”

  1. 1 Classifier Determines the kind of work. The exact kind label is not specified by the essay.
  2. 2 Protocol Supplies the canonical, versioned sequence for that kind so this run can be compared with another.
  3. 3 Compiler + Prompts Applies the protocol to the question and binds versioned instructions, producing the shape a human can inspect and the plan a machine can consume.
  4. 4 Human confirmation First approves the content of the shape, then authorizes firing that same digest. A machine plan with an unconfirmed step fails closed.
  5. 5 Execution Resolves the bound prompt versions at run time, starts the work, and collects what comes back.
Fabric · throughout agent work Mediates every emission, preserves messages, seals judgments, and never authors.
Identity · throughout the system Gives addressable identities and typed relations to everything other artifacts reference.
Record · throughout the run Appends every durable event, including acts with no content. It is the single writer.
Prompts · compile and run time Supplies versioned instruction bindings that Execution resolves when the run fires.

Why these services

Seven guarantees derive the architecture

Essay §4–§5

Make the record exist

Without these, there is no durable, meaningful record to inspect.

  • R1 A thing stays addressable when its description changes.Provided by Identity.
  • R6 What happened is recoverable later.Provided by Record.
  • R7 A connection means something specific.Provided by Identity.

Make the record trustworthy

Without these, the record may be complete but cannot support governance claims.

  • R2 Two runs of the same kind are comparable.Provided by Classifier and Protocol.
  • R3 The confirmed thing and the executed thing are the same thing.Provided by Compiler, Prompts, and Execution.
  • R4 An independent judgment is not visible to peers before it is fixed.Provided by Fabric.
  • R5 What was said is what is read.Provided by Fabric.

Essay-backed distinction The “exist” and “trustworthy” grouping is stated in the essay. “Provided by” means the service is required for the guarantee to hold; it does not mean a service privately owns a guarantee.

Do not mistake the picture for a decision

The proposal still has load-bearing gaps

proposal-only
D3 · CRITICAL

Is confirmation truly binding?

The proposal requires the executed shape to be the exact shape confirmed. If that check is wrong, confirmation becomes theatre and every downstream guarantee inherits the mismatch.

D9 · CRITICAL

Who owns authority?

The architecture says work runs under authority, but none of the eight services provides it. A ninth service may be needed, or authority may belong to Record. The essay leaves this undecided.
