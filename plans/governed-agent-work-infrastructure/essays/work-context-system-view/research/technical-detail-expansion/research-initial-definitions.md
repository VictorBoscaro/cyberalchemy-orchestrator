# Research Initial Definitions — Technical detail expansion

## Context

The Governed Agent Work Infrastructure plan is developing infrastructure that keeps local work
connected to the objectives, decisions, authority, evidence, attempts, effects, and artifacts that
give it meaning. Its work-context system-view companion currently presents that shape from the
human origin of an objective through candidate architecture responsibilities and a first testable
vertical slice.

The current essay is intentionally proposal-only and increases in technical density toward its
end. A completed redundancy review found that its central thesis should remain intact and that no
numbered section was shown to be wholly disposable. The next revision needs to improve navigation,
repair reference and diagram defects, and determine where additional technical detail would make
the proposed system more concrete without turning a system view into an implementation
specification.

## Purpose

This document establishes the informational baseline for research that will inform the next
revision of the work-context system-view essay. The later revision should be able to distinguish
technical detail that clarifies the system's shape and validation strategy from detail that belongs
in an ontology view, engineer view, domain specification, or experiment.

## Research Question (Can be refined)

Which additional technical details would materially improve the explanatory and architectural
value of the work-context system-view essay while preserving its proposal-only authority,
shape-and-stakes altitude, and explicit deferral of definitions, implementation mechanics, and
design verdicts?

## Confirmed Product Constraints

- The target remains a proposal-only system-view companion rather than a canonical ontology,
  engineer view, implementation specification, or runtime authority.
- The central thesis connects intention to effect while keeping purpose, authority, assignment,
  causation, and realization independently inspectable.
- The essay may name load-bearing stances but must not decide the verdicts assigned to future
  ontology or engineer-view owners.
- Existing ACI and APT correspondence may be retained when it is visually identified as current
  repository correspondence rather than a universal dependency.
- The target essay is
  `plans/governed-agent-work-infrastructure/essays/work-context-system-view/essay.md`.
- The next review must use three independent reviewers before an authoring agent changes the
  target, followed by independent review of the actual change.
- The prior review record is
  `plans/governed-agent-work-infrastructure/essays/work-context-system-view/review/review.md`.

## Current Evidence Baseline

- The target essay already inventories candidate responsibilities, ontology deferrals,
  candidate invariants, formalization warrants, open decisions, and a frozen-fixture vertical
  slice.
- The prior review found three load-bearing defects: a dangling companion reference, a late
  statement of the five inspectable paths, and ambiguous graph/history/projection directionality.
- The same review found useful navigation refinements: an early reading map, an explicit boundary
  around modeling human perception, a repository-correspondence subsection for ACI/APT, selective
  compression, and consolidation of overlapping open-work inventories.
- The local `system-view` skill defines the target altitude as narrative shape and stakes, with
  term definitions deferred to an ontology view and stance verdicts deferred to an engineer view.
- The broader plan, predecessor essays, ACI specification, APT specification, and companion
  agent-language system view already supply repository evidence that can constrain additions.

## Known Gaps

- It is not yet clear which technical concepts are missing versus merely introduced too late.
- The appropriate amount of detail for graph families, durable history, projections, path
  witnesses, and composition rules has not been established at system-view altitude.
- The essay does not yet state a compact technical contract for the five inspectable paths near
  its beginning.
- The boundary between a useful first-slice correspondence and implementation-specific ACI/APT
  mechanics needs sharper presentation.
- The relationship among direct facts, accepted assertions, events, durable history, graph views,
  and task-specific projections remains visually and conceptually ambiguous.
- The prior review did not produce a successfully delivered downstream verifier return.
- The cost and benefit of adding examples, failure cases, or formal obligations at each conceptual
  layer has not yet been assessed.
