## Recommended additions

### 1. Bring the five paths forward

After section 3, name purpose, authority, assignment, causation, and realization as independently inspectable paths. State that they may converge on one outcome but cannot be inferred from one another.

Smallest witness: an execution can be authorized but unassigned, so authority cannot supply assignment.

Keep this conceptual. Do not define canonical relation names, sufficient-path rules, schemas, or enforcement behavior.

### 2. Replace the singular graph pipeline with a graph-family shape

Section 29 should show attributable assertions, decisions, and events entering durable history, from which overlapping graph views and task-specific projections can be formed. It must say that persisted, derived, and hybrid ownership remains open.

Smallest witness: the current drawing permits two unintended judgments—that one graph is canonical and that current graph state produces history.

Do not declare a mandatory graph taxonomy, graph membership, edge types, persistence owner, or event-sourcing architecture.

### 3. Join direct/derived separation with replayable path witnesses

Use one compact section 30 addition:

- direct relations are attributable historical source material;
- derived conclusions depend on applicable composition stances;
- a witness explains why a conclusion held then and whether it remains applicable now;
- changing a rule or acceptance can invalidate the conclusion without rewriting its direct sources.

Smallest witness: retained source references cannot explain why a path was valid under an earlier rule and invalid now.

Describe the questions a witness answers, not mandatory fields, records, hashes, or storage strategy.

### 4. State non-manufacture obligations for projections

At the end of section 26, distinguish legitimate omission from semantic invention. A task-specific projection must not manufacture identity, fact, authority, causation, or currentness unsupported by accepted sources.

Smallest witness: a folder tree cannot authorize action, and proximity in a view cannot establish causation.

Leave positive preservation laws, projection functions, completeness classes, and cache policy open.

### 5. Split terminal state from outcome binding

In section 35, explain terminal evidence as two separable obligations:

1. a terminal-state witness;
2. an outcome-binding witness.

A terminal attempt without the second is activity evidence, not causal or realization evidence. A finalized artifact without a producer binding is durable content, not realization evidence. An intent without an accepted outcome observation remains a request.

Smallest witness: terminal activity can exist without evidence that the intended outcome occurred.

Do not prescribe runtime events, receipts, artifact records, observation schemas, or failure codes.

### 6. Localize drift through invalidated dependencies

Sections 24–25 should say that a useful diagnostic identifies the changed source, dependent claim, invalidated assumption, and affected path. It may classify the path as current, missing, conflicted, or superseded; it may not silently repair lineage.

Smallest witness: mutating one accepted dependency should explain which path changed and why, not merely report generic staleness.

Do not select dependency indexes, event stores, hook topology, digest algorithms, or automatic repair.

### 7. Make the first experiment discriminating

Section 36 should start from one fully witnessed golden case and derive negative cases by changing one binding at a time. Check both traversal directions, the first broken binding, and that unrelated paths remain unchanged. Include one cross-domain case where locally valid judgments fail to license their composition.

Measure:

- answer classification and first-break localization;
- investigation time and evidence sources inspected;
- capture burden;
- repair burden;
- false confidence;
- comparison with ordinary repository reconstruction.

Smallest witness: a one-binding mutation assigns a failure to one semantic obligation; capture and repair burden can reverse an adoption decision even if answer quality improves.

Leave fixture encoding, instruments, samples, workload accounting, thresholds, and stopping values to the experiment owner.

## Compact semantic outcomes

The terms `witnessed`, `missing`, `conflicted`, and `superseded` are useful as explanatory distinctions, not a response enum. Introduce them briefly near the five paths, demonstrate them in drift and fixture examples, and avoid calling them a runtime “result contract.”
