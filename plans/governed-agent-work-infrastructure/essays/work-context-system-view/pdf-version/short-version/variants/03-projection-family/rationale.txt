03 - Projection Family

Design rationale

Page 2 - invariant identity under projection
The single vertical spine is the stable referent. Three offset, incomplete plane contours intersect it at different levels, expressing partial projections without turning the planes into orbiting frames. Two hollow nodes and one dark anchor keep the invariant legible while the planes remain almost absorbed into the paper.

Page 3 - a graph family with several views
A durable central history is drawn as one vertical sequence of hollow nodes and small branch marks. Three separate traces project from that history into three spatially distinct, incomplete fields. The fields stand for purpose, authority, and evidence without labels, hierarchy, arrows, or visual collapse into one universal view.

Page 4 - plane alignment
Four abstract plane marks meet one shared horizontal record axis. Their differing silhouettes remain distinguishable while the common axis implies participation in the same run. The four marks suggest decision, execution, mediation, and knowledge without naming or ordering them.

Small visual adjustments

Only the page 2 and page 3 ornamental figures and their local CSS were replaced. One new normal-flow figure was added immediately after .part4-reading on page 4. The original fixed dimensions were retained for pages 2 and 3 (84 x 24 mm and 137 x 42 mm). The page 4 figure is 112 x 16 mm. All three use the existing paper, ink, muted brown, and red colors; hairline strokes; sparse hollow nodes; no labels; no arrows; no external assets; aria-hidden="true"; centered normal-flow placement; and break-inside avoidance.
