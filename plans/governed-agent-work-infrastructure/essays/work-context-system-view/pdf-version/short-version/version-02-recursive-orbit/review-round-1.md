# Review round 1 — Version 02: Recursive Orbit

## Verdict

**Revise.** The eight-page structure, restrained palette, alternating light/dark rhythm, and recursive-composition visual are strong. The PDF renders without visible clipping or overlap. It is not yet ready because Level 4 presents an unresolved authority boundary as settled, and its densest infrastructure text is too small for comfortable print reading.

## Prioritized findings

1. **Critical — do not present “Authority provider” as an established service.** Page 6 says it “proves” effect boundaries and places it alongside the named services. The source x-ray marks authority ownership as **CRITICAL / undecided (D9)**: it may be a ninth service or a Record property. Render this as a dashed/open slot labeled “Authority ownership — unresolved,” and repeat that status beside the diagram, not only in the page-8 caveat.

2. **High — make Level 4 more faithful and explanatory.** Page 6 is a useful responsibility map, but the source’s defining separation—**strategy decision → saved task DAG → interpreted, validated Dispatch**—is compressed into small prose. Promote that transformation into the primary visual and show the two interpretation inputs (agent meaning and system constraints), confirmation/digest boundary, and fail-closed authority gate. Keep supporting services visibly “around” the spine.

3. **High — increase minimum print size in dense areas.** Service descriptions, legend, contracts, diagram annotation, page-5 state labels, and folios fall around 7.1–8.5 pt. At A4 these are technically legible but effortful, especially reversed on dark pages. Target roughly 8.5–9 pt minimum for explanatory copy and simplify text where space is tight; page 6 has ample unused lower space.

4. **Medium — Section 3 needs one concrete traversal example.** The five independent paths are intact and clearly named, but the page remains an improved list rather than an explanatory model. Add a compact example outcome with five separately colored/typed incoming paths, including one deliberately missing path, so “independently answerable” becomes immediately testable rather than only stated.

5. **Medium — tighten page density and graphic consistency.** Pages 6 and 8 leave large inert lower fields while concentrating small content above; page 7 crowds its footer against the closing quote. Redistribute vertically, enlarge the central diagrams, and reuse the orbit/line motif as functional routing geometry. This will preserve the classic minimalist/high-tech character while making the system feel designed rather than typeset.

## Acceptance for round 2

Authority status is explicit in the diagram; the DAG-to-Dispatch boundary is visually inspectable; dense copy is comfortably printable; Section 3 includes a five-path traversal example; and all eight pages still render with zero clipping.
