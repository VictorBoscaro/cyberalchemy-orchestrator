Revision without erasure

Learning should bend the route without cleaning up the past.

The ideaA decision changes at a witnessed point. The earlier path remains faintly available; the present does not pretend it was always inevitable.

Into the systemA revision binds the old state, the new state, the evidence, and the acceptance. Nothing is overwritten.

Why the whole needs itThe system can learn without losing its memory. Later agents can distinguish changed conditions from hidden mistakes.

new evidence enters here
