# Review - work-context short system view

## Review result

Two artifact defects survived both independent verifiers:

1. `context` changes meaning without marking the transition from situated human context to
   recoverable work relationships;
2. the page-2 diagram collapses decomposition, attempts, artifacts, effects, evidence, inheritance,
   and revision into one hierarchy and feedback arrow.

The title change, clarification of `route` and `bounded form of work`, and replacement of the
component architecture remain confirmed revision directions from the owner. They are recorded
separately because at least one verifier refuted their presentation as defects in the current
artifact.

**Overall verdict: FIX.**

## Confirmed revision direction

These are owner decisions and constraints, not severity-ranked review findings:

1. **Change the title.** The revised title should describe the movement from intention through
   distributed work to recoverable continuity. It should not promise an infrastructure overview.
2. **End in infrastructure properties, not a component architecture.** Derive the properties from
   the preceding problem and preserve the modest promise to make forgetting less automatic.
3. **Define only the operational terms that need support.** Mark the meanings of `context`,
   `route`, and `bounded form of work`. Do not add definitions for perception, intention, or
   objective.
4. **Preserve the intended experience.** The reader should move through recognition, quiet unease,
   relief, and cautious trust. The text and design should remain warm, spacious, editorial,
   provisional, and human rather than promotional or technologically triumphant.

## Change requests

1. **MAJOR - mark the operational meaning of context.** Preserve the human use on page 1, then
   explicitly signal the shift to recoverable work relationships.

   realization, and detailed work are equivalent children; that artifacts and effects constitute
   realization; and that context inheritance and evidence-driven revision happen automatically.
