## Mathematical/formal non-vacuity skeptic

**Non-vacuity gate: FLAG.** The repository supports a minimal validation/refinement pattern, but not a representation-induced optimization mechanism.

Positive witness, instantiated from the repository’s CM-05 pattern (`research/agent-language-mathematical-formalization/research.md:162-173`): two locally valid dependencies `a→b` and `b→a` become a globally incompatible 2-cycle after graph assembly. The cycle is typed residue/obstruction; refinement removes or reroutes an edge or introduces a rank constraint. Adding the invented objective `J(G)=|E(G)|` makes retaining one edge optimal, but that objective is not repository-owned.

Closed negative: let `X={x_bad,x_good}`, `D={d}`, and let `q` collapse both source states to `d`. Only `x_good` is truly feasible, yet the compressed state passes its local condition and has an existential feasible lift. No observer of `D` can establish which lift is feasible or optimal. Refinement cannot recover the distinction from `D` alone; the system must enrich `q`, retain a certificate, or require a section/lift preserving feasibility and objective.

Gate conclusion: retain “local checks → representation → joint constraint check → explicit obstruction → refinement.” Do not call it representation-induced optimization without an objective, map, compatibility predicate, residue type, and certified lift/preservation conditions.
