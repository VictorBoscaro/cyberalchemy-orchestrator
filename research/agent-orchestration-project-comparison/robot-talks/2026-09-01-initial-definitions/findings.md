# Robot-Talks findings

These are framing tensions that must be disposed before the initial-definitions document is
finalized. They are not findings about the eight external repositories.

## T1 — The comparison object is state-asymmetric

- **Layer A holds:** the central question compares what external projects provide with what
  Cyberalchemy has and proposes.
- **Layer B actually does:** Cyberalchemy is not one delivered object; its relevant surface includes
  built components, an opt-in local pilot, proposals, open obligations, and contested claims.
- **Severity:** critical — collapsing these states would manufacture false gaps and false parity.
- **Evidence:** `reports/01-cyberalchemy-reality-auditor.md`, Key Findings 1–5;
  `reports/02-comparative-framing-critic.md`, Key Findings 1–2;
  `reports/03-decision-context-editor.md`, Gaps or Inconsistencies 4.
- **Recommended disposition:** real + actionable. Define Cyberalchemy as a deliberately separated
  set of evidence states in the initial definitions.
- **Human disposition:** accepted as recommended on 2026-09-01.

## T2 — Repository-local and governance-first are not both settled constraints

- **Layer A holds:** the session assumption says Cyberalchemy should remain governance/evidence-first
  and repository-local.
- **Layer B actually does:** repository-locality is a current implementation and portability intent,
  while provider/host equivalence remains open; the assumption was explicitly selected for
  challenge rather than confirmed as an immutable product decision.
- **Severity:** high — fixing it prematurely would prejudge the research, while discarding it would
  erase the current architecture.
- **Evidence:** `reports/01-cyberalchemy-reality-auditor.md`, Local Tensions 2;
  `reports/02-comparative-framing-critic.md`, Local Tensions 3;
  `reports/03-decision-context-editor.md`, Gaps or Inconsistencies 2.
- **Recommended disposition:** uncertain. Record current locality and evidence discipline in the
  baseline, but keep their future product status in Known Gaps rather than Confirmed Product
  Constraints.
- **Human disposition:** accepted as recommended on 2026-09-01.

## T3 — The downstream decision and owner are unspecified

- **Layer A holds:** the research should identify ways to strengthen Cyberalchemy and possible reuse.
- **Layer B actually does:** no artifact binds the comparison to one discovery, target-model
  decision, adoption boundary, owner, or authority gate.
- **Severity:** high — without a consumer, “useful” can drift into unbounded feature collection.
- **Evidence:** `reports/03-decision-context-editor.md`, Key Finding 3 and Gaps or Inconsistencies 1;
  `reports/02-comparative-framing-critic.md`, Key Finding 4.
- **Recommended disposition:** real + actionable. State that the research informs later discovery,
  target-model design, and bounded adoption decisions, while explicitly granting none of them
  implementation authority.
- **Human disposition:** accepted as recommended on 2026-09-01.

## T4 — The evidence rule is stronger than the preserved candidate baseline

- **Layer A holds:** README claims do not prove a capability.
- **Layer B actually does:** the eight candidates were selected through a preliminary GitHub/README
  sweep, and the original dialogue initially preserved identifiers but not the summaries or their
  evidence status.
- **Severity:** medium — the shortlist is adequate as prior orientation, not as capability evidence.
- **Evidence:** `reports/02-comparative-framing-critic.md`, Gaps or Inconsistencies 1 and Local
  Tensions 1; `dialogue.md`, Prior candidate basis.
- **Recommended disposition:** real + actionable. Preserve the shortlist as a preliminary,
  README-level current evidence baseline and treat project capabilities as unknown until the later
  research.
- **Human disposition:** accepted as recommended on 2026-09-01.

## T5 — Code reuse is named, but the receiving license is absent

- **Layer A holds:** reusable code must have compatible licensing and implementation fit.
- **Layer B actually does:** Cyberalchemy has no declared `LICENSE`, so compatibility with its future
  distribution terms cannot presently be concluded.
- **Severity:** high for code adoption; low for learning from concepts and patterns.
- **Evidence:** `reports/01-cyberalchemy-reality-auditor.md`, Gaps or Inconsistencies 3;
  `reports/03-decision-context-editor.md`, Gaps or Inconsistencies 3 and Local Tensions 3.
- **Recommended disposition:** real + deferred. Keep code reuse as a research subject, but record
  actual code adoption as blocked on a declared receiving license and a later license review.
- **Human disposition:** accepted as recommended on 2026-09-01.

## T6 — “Mandatory” enforcement is host-bounded and currently contested

- **Layer A holds:** the root overview describes mandatory hooks that deny launch when the governed
  opening fails.
- **Layer B actually does:** enforcement depends on a trusted hook-capable host, and the active
  work-pack records a Codex bypass pending reload and live smoke evidence.
- **Severity:** high — this changes the proven Cyberalchemy baseline used in comparison.
- **Evidence:** `reports/01-cyberalchemy-reality-auditor.md`, Gaps or Inconsistencies 1–2 and Local
  Tensions 4.
- **Recommended disposition:** real + actionable. Record the mechanism as built but host-bounded and
  currently contested; do not present host-independent governance as delivered.
- **Human disposition:** accepted as recommended on 2026-09-01.

## T7 — Comparative learning must not become design authority

- **Layer A holds:** external projects may reveal useful capabilities, patterns, and implementations.
- **Layer B actually does:** the draft target-model branch says the intended system should be derived
  from first principles rather than predefined by existing artifacts.
- **Severity:** medium — precedent can inform a decision without owning it.
- **Evidence:** `reports/03-decision-context-editor.md`, Local Tensions 1 and Key Finding 5.
- **Recommended disposition:** real + actionable. Treat external projects as evidence and precedent,
  not as authority over Cyberalchemy’s target model.
- **Human disposition:** accepted as recommended on 2026-09-01.

## One-line synthesis

The comparative research is warranted, but its initial definitions must separate evidence states,
avoid treating current locality as a fixed product law, name its downstream informational purpose,
and prevent external precedent or README claims from becoming design authority.
