# Research Initial Definitions - Knowledge Formation

## Context

`cyberalchemy-orchestrator` develops ways to preserve the relationships, evidence, authority, and
history needed to understand and govern what agents and people produce. Its current materials can
already distinguish information, evidence, acceptance, provenance, and knowledge for architectural
purposes. Those distinctions begin at the point where something has been produced or may be reused;
they do not yet provide a slow account of how phenomena that may later be called knowledge become
discernible in experience, practice, and social life.

The immediate problem is therefore prior to system design. A future reader-facing essay should be
able to approach knowledge without importing the conceptual path of the existing essay about work,
without presuming that knowledge begins with an explicit question, and without treating perception,
information, learning, understanding, belief, justification, memory, or transmission as synonyms.
Establishing an honest field of uncertainty matters because an elegant narrative sequence could
otherwise be mistaken for a general account of how knowledge forms.

## Purpose

This document establishes the informational context, confirmed boundaries, existing evidence, and
known gaps for a broad investigation of knowledge formation. The research will inform the later
decision about the conceptual starting point and first movement of an independent phenomenological
essay about knowledge. It may also clarify which distinctions a future knowledge system would need
to preserve, but it does not specify that system or authorize the essay's structure.

## Research Question (Can be refined)

What experiential, cognitive, social, and epistemic movements may participate in knowledge
acquiring form, and which distinctions must remain open so that a phenomenological introduction
does not impose a universal origin, sequence, subject, or definition of knowledge?

This wording is provisional. The research may refine the question if the internal corpus or
external scholarship shows that `formation`, `movement`, or the assumed unit called `knowledge`
already combines importantly different phenomena.

## Confirmed Product Constraints

- The research must be broad enough to examine both the repository's existing conceptual material
  and relevant external scholarship.
- Internal and external evidence belong to the same investigation; neither is automatically the
  authority through which the other must be classified.
- The future knowledge essay must be conceptually independent from the existing work essay. It must
  not mention work, objectives, tasks, agents, execution, or infrastructure in order to establish
  its own opening movement.
- The work and knowledge movements may be related only in a later artifact, after each has been
  developed from its own starting conditions.
- An explicit question must not be assumed to be the universal beginning of knowledge.
- The point at which the future essay begins must not be presented as the point at which knowledge
  itself begins. An expository entry point is not an origin claim.
- Information, learning, understanding, belief, memory, skill, knowing how, tacit understanding,
  justification, confidence, testimony, and knowledge must not be treated as interchangeable,
  mutually exclusive kinds, or successive stages merely to produce a smooth narrative.
- Distinctions introduced for inquiry may overlap, co-constitute one another, or require revision;
  distinguishing them does not establish that they are separate stages.
- Narrative order must not be presented as an ontological, psychological, historical, or
  epistemological order without commensurate evidence.
- The research must not assume a universal subject or unit of knowledge, including an isolated
  individual. Possible individual, relational, practical, social, institutional, or distributed
  subjects and units, and the relations among them, remain unresolved.
- The word `knowledge` may appear in the future essay, but the opening must not depend on a premature
  exhaustive definition of it.
- The existing short work-context PDF may later guide pacing, page composition, and family
  resemblance only. Its conceptual sequence is not a template for the knowledge account.
- The later visual edition should use a blue palette and a parameterized theme tool, but visual
  production is outside this research's present authority.

## Known Gaps

- The phenomenon, relation, or change, if any, that provides an appropriate expository entry into
  knowledge remains unresolved.
- The relations among phenomena relevant to knowledge formation, and whether they admit a common
  organization at all, remain unresolved.
- The boundaries among receiving information, learning, understanding, knowing how, knowing that,
  remembering, believing, and being justified remain unresolved for this purpose.
- It is unclear when a change in a person, embodied capacity, shared practice, testimonial
  relation, institution, or distributed arrangement counts as learning or contributes to
  knowledge, and whether either learning or knowledge must be represented as a state possessed by
  a person.
- The roles of embodiment, affect, habit, skill, tacit understanding, language, and prior knowledge
  in making new distinctions available have not been established.
- The possible subjects or units to which knowledge may be attributed, and the relations or
  co-constitution among them, remain unresolved.
- It is unclear what permanence means for knowledge: retention in memory, repeatable ability,
  reliable orientation, public record, social acceptance, revisability, or something else.
- Transmission may preserve a statement while changing or losing the conditions under which it was
  understood. The significance of that difference remains open.
- The relationship of testimony to the formation, attribution, and transmission of knowledge
  remains unresolved.
- It is not known whether preserving a statement while losing memory, skill, practice, access,
  records, institutional capacity, or reliable orientation preserves the relevant knowledge at all.
- The existing repository may use the word `knowledge` for several purposes that are compatible,
  nested, or in tension. Their genealogy and boundaries have not yet been reconstructed.
- Relevant external traditions, disagreements, and established distinctions have not yet been
  mapped, so the project has no basis for claiming that its current framing is complete, original,
  or appropriately typed.
- It is not known where the future essay can first use the word `knowledge` without either hiding
  the phenomenon behind a definition or allowing the word to mean everything that precedes it.
- The eventual relation between the independently developed movements of work and knowledge remains
  intentionally undetermined.
