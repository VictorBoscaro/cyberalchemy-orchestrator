The repository has a coherent protocol for governing explicit epistemic artifacts: preserve source occurrences, distinguish readouts from interpretation and evidence, retain provenance, append revisions, and prevent transport from silently authoring content. This supports inspectable reuse, but it does not yet establish an account of embodied skill, tacit integration, learning as change in a knower, or recipient uptake.

My dissent is that exact attributed transmission preserves an utterance, not necessarily the understanding that made it usable. The architecture should keep recipient-side interpretation and “uptake unknown” distinct rather than infer shared knowledge from identical payloads, provenance, or acceptance.
