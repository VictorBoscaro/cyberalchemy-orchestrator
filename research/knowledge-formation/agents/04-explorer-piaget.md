Phase B recommends revising, not replacing, the initial framing. Its rejection of a universal origin, compulsory stages, synonymy among epistemic terms, and an isolated default bearer is supported. The governing question remains underspecified because it folds formation or reorganization, recognition or attribution, epistemic warrant, and stabilization or preservation into “knowledge acquiring form.” These registers can interact but cannot substitute for one another without evidence.

The corrected frame should type claims by content or competence, bearer, attribution criterion, timescale, mechanism, setting and culture, persistence or failure mode, normative standard, and evidence. It should also test competence against performance conditions, context and transfer, conceptual coexistence, testimony and source evaluation, tacit knowing, motivation, and distributed accomplishment.

Dissent: adding more factors to an untyped inventory is insufficient. Without the four-register split and explicit claim typing, the account remains vulnerable to treating better evidence as newly formed competence, institutional acceptance as warrant, or preserved statements as preserved understanding.
