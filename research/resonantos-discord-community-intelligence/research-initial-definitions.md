# Initial definitions: ResonantOS Discord community intelligence

## Context

ResonantOS develops infrastructure that keeps community work connected to its objectives,
decisions, assumptions, actions, and evidence. A small community-intelligence hub could make
basic signals about participation, integration, and activity in the ResonantOS Discord community
more visible.

The local problem is to determine which Discord information could responsibly support that hub
without promising unavailable metrics, requiring disproportionate collection, or producing
invasive interpretations about community participants.

## Purpose

This document establishes the informational boundary for research that will inform a later
decision about which Discord information and metrics should be included in an initial version of
the ResonantOS community-intelligence hub.

## Research Question (Can be refined)

Which information useful for understanding the ResonantOS community can be responsibly obtained
or derived through Discord's official interfaces, and what permissions, limitations, collection
costs, and privacy implications constrain its use?

## Confirmed Product Constraints

- The intended product is a small intelligence hub for ResonantOS.
- The initial focus is basic information about the community.
- Discord is the platform in scope.
- Metric selection must consider both product usefulness and technical feasibility.
- Desirable metrics must not be treated as available until their availability is verified.
- An initial version should favor reliable and understandable indicators.

## Current Evidence Baseline

- Initial product questions concern growth, participation, integration, responsiveness, topics,
  and community initiatives.
- No verification against Discord's official interfaces or documentation has yet been performed.

## Known Gaps

- It is unknown which relevant data is available through Discord's REST API, Gateway, or other
  official interfaces.
- It is unknown which data can be recovered retroactively and which must be observed and stored
  after installation.
- The required permissions and intents have not been established.
- The technical limits affecting accuracy, completeness, and collection cost are unknown.
- It is unclear which useful metrics are directly available and which would need to be derived.
- The privacy and proportionality boundaries for community-content analysis have not been set.
- The intended consumers of the hub and the community decisions or actions its information should
  support have not been defined.
- The boundary of the Discord environment included in the analysis has not been established.
- The population of accounts and members included in the analysis has not been delimited.
- The smallest useful set of metrics for an initial version remains undecided.
