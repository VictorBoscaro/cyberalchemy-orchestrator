## Method and provenance

Dispatch `2026-08-04-repository-irreducible-problems-exploration-r4` ran three independent perspectives after two failed launch attempts were closed without accepting their outputs. The successful seats covered: objective and collective judgment; authority, provenance, and licensed composition; and execution, observation, evaluation, and realization. The reports completed and their host workflow bindings reached `resolved`; this document consolidates their source-grounded content without promoting proposal-only or private material.

## Perspective 1 — objective, meaning, and collective judgment

The first report found four candidates that do not immediately collapse:

1. loss of the normative connection between decomposed work and its objective;
2. degradation of collective judgment through correlated bias, noise, and framing;
3. semantic indeterminacy or loss between intention and execution;
4. absence of verifiable correspondence and revision between intention and reality.

The repository policy supplies the strongest local formulation: locally correct work is insufficient when it no longer serves its larger purpose (`AGENTS.md:13-21`). The root plan separately frames correlated bias, noise, and framing as a business problem, but remains `proposal-only` (`plans/governed-agent-work-infrastructure/PLAN.md:1-15,66-88`). The local essay proposes typed warrants and licensed paths, yet explicitly leaves its unification and self-similarity claims unsettled (`docs/essays/what-this-is-for/essay.md:27-45,152-184`). The sibling's candidate architecture distinguishes precise intention from observed reality and from promotion (`../domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy/agentic-system-deep-dive.md:41-70,100-188,374-518`).

The report's structural hypothesis was:

```text
comprehensible objective
→ semantically licensed decomposition
→ sufficiently independent judgment
→ execution linked to authority
→ observation of reality
→ comparison and revision
```

This is a candidate chain, not evidence of completeness.

## Perspective 2 — authority, provenance, compatibility, and composition

The second report kept four failures provisionally separate:

1. **provenance loss:** origin or transformation history disappears;
2. **authority drift:** a claim or action counts outside its authorized owner, scope, surface, or operation;
3. **semantic incompatibility:** locally valid objects cannot be jointly used under their applicable types, versions, or constraints;
4. **unlicensed composition:** a conclusion is derived through a path with no accepted and witnessed composition rule.

The evidence explicitly separates provenance from authority: a complete provenance chain may end in a false, private, unpromoted, or unusable claim (`../domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy-v2/authority/definitions/DEFINITIONS.md:2241-2264,2285-2290`). It also separates authority from truth, confidence, maturity, access, and implementation success (`../domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy-v2/authority/definitions/DEFINITIONS.md:2180-2226`). Compatibility and license are distinct because constraints on occupants differ from connection rules between kinds (`../domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy-v2/authority/definitions/DEFINITIONS.md:3918-3963`). A path such as `Task authorized-by Decision; Decision part-of Plan` does not license the inference that the Plan authorized the Task (`plans/governed-agent-work-infrastructure/essays/work-context-system-view/essay.md:701-727`).

Standing remained bounded: the orchestrator README is draft; the root plan and architecture essays are proposals; CyberAlchemy v2 definitions are active only within their declared sibling scope; `AUTHORITY-KINDS.md` is candidate; and private validation material is evidence, not public or normative authority (`README.md:14-18`; `../domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy-v2/SOURCE-MAP.md:18-33`; `../domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy-v2/authority/AUTHORITY-MODEL.md:1-5,74-88,122-151`).

## Perspective 3 — execution, evidence, and realization

The third report rejected “failure of realization” as an atomic problem. It decomposed the result into:

1. intention that is not sufficiently defined or evaluable;
2. absence of a capability able to realize it;
3. execution divergent from accepted authority or context;
4. incomplete observation;
5. evaluation unable to compare evidence with intention;
6. correction that does not alter future behavior;
7. loss of continuity or recovery.

The evidence chain proposed by the report is:

```text
typed intention → confirmed dispatch → effective context → executed effect
→ raw output → accepted contribution → immutable facts → projection
→ comparison → correction → closure verification → governed update
```

The ACI specifications distinguish provider output, accepted contribution, immutable journal facts, and projections (`docs/features/agents-communication-infra/specs/architecture.md:288-302`; `docs/features/agents-communication-infra/specs/workflows.md:83-116`). Observation produces signals, while reflection proposes changes without directly promoting them (`framework/observability/README.md:51-67`; `../domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy/agentic-system-deep-dive.md:723-755`). Memory preserves continuity but does not itself own source or ontological authority (`../domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy/agentic-system-deep-dive.md:619-663`).

The runtime evidence also matters: the Stage F documentation records that this Codex build can spawn without firing `PreToolUse`; therefore a new session must demonstrate state and receipts before results are accepted (`docs/features/agent-provenance-telemetry/integration/stage-f/mandatory-host-wrapper.md:97-112`). This exploration encountered that exact gap and succeeded only after direct bridge binding and verification.

## Cross-perspective agreement

- Local correctness does not imply continuity of purpose.
- Traceable provenance does not imply authority.
- Authority does not imply truth or semantic compatibility.
- Compatibility does not imply a licensed derivation.
- Precise intention does not imply realization.
- Execution does not imply observation, adjudication, correction, or learning.
- Inventory, ontology, ledgers, typed edges, gates, promotion, and observability are mechanisms or required properties; they must not be counted automatically as problems.

## Cross-perspective tension

The objective perspective recommends starting synthesis with four broad candidates: continuity of purpose, quality of judgment, semantic integrity, and correspondence/revision. The authority perspective argues that provenance, authority, compatibility, and composition have operational counterexamples that keep them separate. The execution perspective further decomposes correspondence/revision into intention, capability, execution, observation, adjudication, corrective closure, and continuity. Synthesis must therefore decide the correct altitude: broad failure families versus independently falsifiable failure mechanisms.

## Collapse tests carried forward

1. Preserve objective linkage while introducing correlated judgment error; if failure remains, judgment does not collapse into purpose continuity.
2. Preserve provenance while withholding promotion or scope authority; if use remains forbidden, authority does not collapse into provenance.
3. Supply semantic compatibility without an owner-approved connection rule; if derivation remains blocked, license does not collapse into compatibility.
4. Supply a precise specification and faithful execution but omit observation; if realization cannot be adjudicated, observation remains independent.
5. Supply complete evidence but no comparison criterion; if no verdict follows, evaluation remains independent from observation.
6. Detect a mismatch without changing future governed behavior; if recurrence remains possible, corrective closure remains independent from detection.
7. Preserve authoritative journal facts while removing a separate memory layer; if continuity remains reconstructible, memory may collapse into provenance and projection.
8. For every candidate, remove only its presumed response property; if no distinct failure becomes observable, the candidate is probably a mechanism or property re-labelled as a problem.
