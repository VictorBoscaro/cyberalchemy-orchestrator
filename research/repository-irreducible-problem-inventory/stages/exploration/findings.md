## Verdict

The exploration found no defensible single “fundamental problem” and did not establish a complete irreducible inventory. It found a layered candidate structure in which broad failure families contain mechanisms that may remain independently falsifiable.

## Candidate families

1. **Continuity of purpose:** decomposed work can remain locally correct while losing the objective, decision, premise, or evidence that justified it (`AGENTS.md:13-21`).
2. **Quality of collective judgment:** preserved context does not prevent correlated bias, framing, anchoring, or premature consensus (`plans/governed-agent-work-infrastructure/PLAN.md:66-88,190-207`).
3. **Semantic integrity:** traceability can faithfully preserve an intention that is vague, contradictory, incompatible, or translated with loss (`../domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy/agentic-system-deep-dive.md:146-188`).
4. **Authority and licensed use:** provenance, authority, compatibility, and composition have counterexamples that prevent immediate collapse (`../domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy-v2/authority/definitions/DEFINITIONS.md:2180-2226,2241-2290,3918-3963`).
5. **Realization and corrective closure:** precise intent and faithful execution do not guarantee observable evidence, adjudication, correction, learning, or continuity (`../domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy/agentic-system-deep-dive.md:100-144,619-663,695-755`).

## Strongest provisional distinctions

- Provenance is not authority.
- Authority is not truth, evidence, or implementation success.
- Semantic compatibility is not permission to compose.
- Observation is not evaluation.
- Evaluation is not corrective closure.
- Failure of realization is a result class, not yet an irreducible problem; only a narrower non-realizability claim may survive.

## Exclusions from the problem inventory

Inventory, ontology, memory stores, node envelopes, ledgers, typed edges, gates, promotion, observability, validators, compilers, and projections are currently response mechanisms or required properties. “Residue” remains an overloaded diagnostic term and should not be promoted to an irreducible problem until its tested meaning is fixed.

## Unresolved questions

- What altitude should define irreducibility: broad failure family or smallest independently falsifiable mechanism?
- Does recoverable journal provenance eliminate a separate memory problem?
- When is evidence sufficient to adjudicate realization?
- Is absence of capability distinct from divergent execution in controlled negative cases?
- Which sibling definitions can be reused as precedent, and which require a local owner decision before they govern the orchestrator?

## Required next stage

A synthesizer should build a candidate dependency graph from these findings, apply every collapse test, retain standing on each cited claim, and emit explicit `survives`, `collapses-into`, or `remains-open` verdicts. Independent skeptics should then attack completeness, category errors, authority transfer, and counterexamples before any final inventory is promoted.
