# Research Initial Definitions — Epistemic Repository Bootstrap

## Context

The surrounding repositories separate three kinds of work: agent-governance and explanatory
infrastructure in `cyberalchemy-orchestrator`, machine-checked categorical results and research
audits in `domainspec-lean-formalization`, and philosophical writing plus Victor's publication voice
in `business-philosopher`.

The present work supports a new repository intended to keep an emerging line of inquiry coherent
across formalization, conceptual development, visual explanation, and publication. The immediate
need is to understand which existing assets and constraints should inform that repository's first
plan without moving or rewriting the source repositories.

## Purpose

This document establishes the context for a bounded repository reconnaissance. Its findings will
inform the new repository's name, boundary, initial structure, first formalization sequence, and
representation surfaces.

## Research Question (Can be refined)

Which proven artifacts, open obligations, explanatory tools, and voice conventions from the three
source repositories should constrain the first plan for a new repository devoted to developing and
testing the emerging inquiry?

## Confirmed Product Constraints

- Create a new local repository on this computer after the scout proposal is confirmed.
- Preserve the three existing repositories as read-only sources during reconnaissance.
- The first deliverable in the new repository is a plan, not a completed theory.
- The plan must distinguish existing Lean proof, interpretation, hypothesis, and future proof
  obligations.
- The repository should support Lean development, concise texts, visual x-rays, PDF-ready versions,
  and related efficient representations.
- Victor's writing voice may inform publication surfaces, but raw exploration and published prose
  remain distinct.
- Scouts may inspect `cyberalchemy-orchestrator`, `../domainspec-lean-formalization`, and
  `../business-philosopher`.

## Current Evidence Baseline

- `domainspec-lean-formalization` contains a machine-checked categorical spine, prior research on
  Yoneda and discovery, and explicit claim-versus-proof boundaries.
- `cyberalchemy-orchestrator` contains governed research workflows, hypothesis artifacts, x-ray
  explanation infrastructure, and document-oriented representation patterns.
- `business-philosopher` contains Victor's voice constitution, writing guidance, and examples of
  moving from concrete experience to restrained structural claims.
- The current conversation has clarified that the inquiry concerns how lenses, distinctions,
  identifications, bridges, instances, and schemas interact in scientific knowledge. This is an
  initial framing, not an accepted formal model.

## Known Gaps

- The new repository's name and exact ownership boundary are undecided.
- It is unclear which existing Lean artifacts can be reused directly and which require new
  definitions or bridge lemmas.
- The strongest non-tautological formal statement corresponding to the inquiry is not yet known.
- The minimal useful visual and publication stack has not been selected.
- The relationship between internal research prose and Victor-voice publication prose needs an
  explicit boundary.
- No first-plan structure, milestone sequence, or acceptance boundary has yet been agreed.
