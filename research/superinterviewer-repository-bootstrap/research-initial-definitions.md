# Research Initial Definitions — Superinterviewer Repository Bootstrap

## Context

The Superinterviewer is intended to be the person's primary interface and intellectual partner while work is being formed and carried out. It receives incomplete intentions and may ask, inform, suggest, reframe, introduce or compose lenses, preserve unresolved residues, and help determine a sufficiently clear next step while keeping the system's own intervention intent governable.

The project currently exists across a master prompt, conversation history, research artifacts, formalization candidates, orchestration infrastructure, and neighboring repositories. A clean repository is needed so the Superinterviewer can become the explicit product and research authority without silently inheriting accidental boundaries, duplicated machinery, or premature implementation choices from any one source repository.

## Purpose

This document establishes the informational starting point for research that will inform the decision about how to initialize the Superinterviewer repository: what minimum authoritative context it must contain, what scaffold and repository rules it requires at inception, and which existing creation mechanisms or precedents are safe to reuse.

## Research Question (Can be refined)

What is the smallest sound, usable, and governable repository foundation for beginning the Superinterviewer as both the primary human-facing partner and the owner of its large integration research program, while reusing existing infrastructure without importing its accidental architecture?

## Confirmed Product Constraints

- The Superinterviewer, not a generic framing framework, is the primary product and repository focus.
- It is the person's main interface and working partner, not merely a requirements interviewer or question-only chatbot.
- Its interventions include asking, bringing information, suggesting, and proposing or testing reframings and lenses.
- The repository must support a large research program across existing repositories and external literature before prematurely fixing the complete system design.
- The new starting point should be clean; the complete program should not be developed inside `cyberalchemy-orchestrator`.
- Existing work should be evaluated for reuse rather than copied or migrated wholesale before its fit is understood.
- Claims stronger than their evidence, silent replacement of user intention, and accidental resolution of open product questions are not acceptable.
- Product charter, master research context, research plan, scoped investigations, findings, and decisions must remain distinct authorities.
- The initial repository must not apply the complete `mint` package; only evidenced mold elements may be reused proportionally.
- `subagent-work-infrastructure` is a peer/provider candidate, not a subordinate module or the owner of Superinterviewer product concepts.
- Initial dependencies must use narrow pinned references or contracts; broad framework imports are default-denied.
- Product implementation requires a prior discriminating research or experiment gate that names the uncertainty and acceptance evidence.

## Current Evidence Baseline

- The supplied `PROMPT-MESTRE — SUPERINTERVIEWER, REFINAMENTO DE INTENÇÃO E FRAMING ADAPTATIVO` describes the current product direction, epistemic labels, interaction model, research agenda, major refutations, and desired founding-document shape.
- `cyberalchemy-orchestrator` contains repository-governance machinery, research workflows, subagent dispatch infrastructure, and accumulated precedents, but it is not intended to remain the clean product home.
- `../subagent-work-infrastructure` is oriented toward governed execution, workflow graphs, dispatch, communication, observability, and provenance; this is relevant infrastructure but does not by itself establish ownership of the Superinterviewer product.
- The local `mint` capability describes a governed authority-spine casting for a new repository, but declares itself intended-not-shipped, non-executed on a live mint, and subject to open signer and public/private gates.
- A separate initial-definition artifact concerning Yoneda and Selmer/local–global framing exists in `../domainspec-lean-formalization`; it supports one formalization branch and is not the founding definition of the whole Superinterviewer.

## Known Gaps

- Which existing repository scaffold is executable and appropriate today is not established.
- The minimum founding artifacts and rules needed before broader research starts are not yet agreed.
- The correct boundary between product authority, research evidence, execution infrastructure, reusable capabilities, and formalization remains incomplete.
- It is unclear which repository-creation precedents are current, superseded, experimental, or tied to assumptions inappropriate for the Superinterviewer.
- The minimum governance spine that protects intent and epistemic status without burdening an early research repository is unknown.
- The required relationship to `subagent-work-infrastructure`, DomainSpec, Arcanum, `cyberalchemy-orchestrator`, and formalization repositories has not been expressed as a concrete import or dependency policy.
- Naming, visibility, licensing, attachment mode, language/toolchain defaults, and the earliest validation checks remain undecided.
