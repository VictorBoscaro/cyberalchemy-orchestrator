# Key Findings

- **The minimum product authority is a repository-owned product charter, not a generic framing framework.** It must preserve the Superinterviewer as the primary interface and intellectual partner, with incomplete intention as its input and a sufficiently clear next step as its outcome; asking, informing and reframing are provisional core moves, while integration, suggestion, lens branching/composition and user rejection/contestability are behavioral obligations that cannot be lost when capabilities are reused. The bootstrap definitions already make this product focus a confirmed constraint, and the Prompt-Mestre makes both the interface identity and its unresolved status explicit. Evidence: `research/superinterviewer-repository-bootstrap/research-initial-definitions.md:5-7,19-25`; `C:/Users/victo/.codex/attachments/887a2607-116c-4ce0-88ff-dbf643e71469/pasted-text.txt:34-40,48-66,133-164,238-251,506-540`.

- **A clean new repository is a defensible provisional boundary, not an established necessity.** The current evidence supports separating product/research authority from `cyberalchemy-orchestrator` and evaluating sibling work before reuse, but it does not compare a new repository against a clean authority package, subtree or other isolation mechanism. The bootstrap artifact itself says both that a clean repository is needed and that scaffold, minimum artifacts, boundary, governance spine and dependency policy remain unknown. Synthesis should therefore preserve “new repo” as the current decision to test, rather than laundering it into evidence. Evidence: `research/superinterviewer-repository-bootstrap/research-initial-definitions.md:7,11-15,22-24,35-43`.

- **A master `research-initial-definitions.md` plus subordinate initial definitions is sound only as a scope hierarchy, not as the whole research authority.** The master can own the umbrella context, product constraints, evidence baseline, known gaps and the rule that branch research cannot redefine the product silently. Each subordinate artifact should own one separable, refinable research question and state what broader questions it excludes. This pattern is supported by the formalization branch, which explicitly limits itself to one possible Yoneda/Selmer relationship, disclaims a broader unification program and does not authorize implementation. However, the artifact contract explicitly says initial definitions are informational context and must not prescribe methods, workstreams, success/stopping conditions, outputs or handoffs. Evidence: `.agents/skills/research-initial-definitions/SKILL.md:8-10,33-46,48-59`; `C:/Users/victo/domainspec-lean-formalization/research/yoneda-selmer-local-global-framing/research-initial-definitions.md:3-15,17-27`.

- **`research-plan` is the correct orchestration layer for the research program, but its authority contract is still provisional.** The local precedent places purpose, research questions, scope, evidence sample, method, evidence standards, outputs, stopping conditions, handoff order and intentionally open decisions in the plan—exactly the material forbidden from initial definitions. Yet the same precedent warns that `research-plan` is only a candidate canonical kind whose producer, authority rollup and permitted relations remain open. The new repository therefore needs an explicit plan-level rule: plans sequence and constrain research; they do not override product decisions, convert hypotheses into facts or absorb branch findings without synthesis. Evidence: `research/resonantos-meetings/research-plan.md:15-17,19-31,61-90,92-135,137-163,175-201`; `.agents/skills/research-initial-definitions/SKILL.md:48-59`.

- **“Research-first” should be an explicit evidence gate against architecture-by-implementation, not a ban on eventual prototypes.** The Prompt-Mestre demands adversarial refutations, discriminating minimal experiments, an evidence matrix, experiment backlog, separation of decided/open matters and explicit protection against questions being answered accidentally by implementation. The minimum authority spine should therefore keep product decisions, hypotheses, analogies, evidence and open questions distinct; require branch findings to cite what they support; and require a synthesis/decision step before they alter the charter or authorize implementation. Evidence: `C:/Users/victo/.codex/attachments/887a2607-116c-4ce0-88ff-dbf643e71469/pasted-text.txt:16-28,548-566,583-613`; `research/superinterviewer-repository-bootstrap/research-initial-definitions.md:22-25`.

# Gaps or Inconsistencies

- No artifact currently names the precedence and mutation rules among product charter, master initial definitions, research plan, subordinate initial definitions, branch findings and product decisions. Without that rule, “master” can mean context owner, editorial owner or truth authority—three materially different roles. Evidence: `research/superinterviewer-repository-bootstrap/research-initial-definitions.md:35-43`; `.agents/skills/research-initial-definitions/SKILL.md:58-59`.
- The intervention taxonomy is not fully reconciled. The bootstrap definitions list asking, bringing information, suggesting, and reframing/lenses, while the Prompt-Mestre provisionally reduces the core to asking, informing and reframing and leaves hypothesis-proposal classification open. “Suggest” must be classified as a cross-cutting speech act or a fourth intervention before becoming a product invariant. Evidence: `research/superinterviewer-repository-bootstrap/research-initial-definitions.md:19-21`; `C:/Users/victo/.codex/attachments/887a2607-116c-4ce0-88ff-dbf643e71469/pasted-text.txt:131-164`.
- The proposed master/subordinate structure lacks admission criteria: when a question deserves a branch, who approves its scope, how overlaps are handled, and how contradictory branch findings roll up without premature reconciliation.
- The case for a separate Git repository lacks comparative evidence and a falsification condition. Current text asserts cleanliness as needed while also admitting that the appropriate executable scaffold and reuse boundaries are unknown. Evidence: `research/superinterviewer-repository-bootstrap/research-initial-definitions.md:7,23-24,35-43`.
- The Prompt-Mestre specifies a rich final founding document, but there is no stated rule for which sections become stable product authority versus living research synthesis. Treating the entire requested output as canonical would freeze hypotheses and mathematical candidates alongside decisions. Evidence: `C:/Users/victo/.codex/attachments/887a2607-116c-4ce0-88ff-dbf643e71469/pasted-text.txt:585-613`.

# Local Tensions

- **Primary interface vs. research-program owner:** the repo must own a large integration program without becoming a research-framework repo in which the human-facing partner is merely one downstream consumer. Product authority should constrain research questions; research findings may revise that authority only through an explicit decision.
- **“Master” authority vs. informational artifact:** a master initial-definitions file can govern scope and lineage, but the current artifact contract denies it plan, findings, specification and runtime authority. Calling it the sole master authority would contradict its defined semantics. Evidence: `.agents/skills/research-initial-definitions/SKILL.md:8-10,48-59`.
- **Research-first vs. empirical learning:** forbidding implementation now protects open questions, but the Prompt-Mestre ultimately requires minimal discriminating experiments and a smallest real prototype. The gate should be “no architecture commitment before evidence and decision,” not “no prototype ever.” Evidence: `C:/Users/victo/.codex/attachments/887a2607-116c-4ce0-88ff-dbf643e71469/pasted-text.txt:566,608-613`.
- **Reuse vs. inherited ownership:** sibling repositories can supply evidence, tools or bounded capabilities, but their orchestration/formalization concerns must not become the Superinterviewer’s product decomposition. The formalization precedent models the right asymmetry by serving one bounded branch and explicitly excluding the broader program. Evidence: `research/superinterviewer-repository-bootstrap/research-initial-definitions.md:29-33`; `C:/Users/victo/domainspec-lean-formalization/research/yoneda-selmer-local-global-framing/research-initial-definitions.md:7-11,25-27`.
- **Unified companion vs. plural lenses:** the product promises one relatively unified interface while rejecting a total lens and allowing conflict, order effects and parallel frames. The charter must make “unified” a continuity/interaction promise, not a promise of one internally coherent worldview. Evidence: `C:/Users/victo/.codex/attachments/887a2607-116c-4ce0-88ff-dbf643e71469/pasted-text.txt:236-262,504-516`.

# Questions for Synthesis

- Is “new repository” already a human decision to record, or a hypothesis that the foundation research must compare against lighter authority-isolation options?
- Which artifact is the final authority for product identity and intervention invariants: a dedicated product charter/founding definition, or a stable section projected from the master research context?
- Can synthesis adopt this minimal split: product charter owns identity and non-negotiable interaction protections; master initial definitions owns umbrella context and branch boundaries; `research-plan` owns sequence, evidence standards and stopping; subordinate initial definitions own bounded questions; findings support an explicit decision register but never mutate the charter automatically?
- What exact relation and conflict rule should connect subordinate findings to the master program when branches disagree or expose that the original product frame is wrong?
- Which result would falsify the need for a separate repository, and which result would authorize moving from research-only artifacts to the first discriminating prototype?
