# Pair 03 — Authority

## Executive answer

The runtime enforces local scope, integrity, and some recipient-continuity rules, but it does not establish an independently authenticated chain of authority. It can show what authority was claimed and what token was consumed; it cannot yet prove that the claimant had the right to authorize the work.

Exact follow-up recipient continuity is proven only when the prior terminal turn has a non-null agent identity. Declared filesystem, tool, model, network, and no-spawn limits remain requested constraints rather than observed fences. At the recorded observation time, the inspected local-pilot store contained no binding row for this dispatch.

## What the next work buys

The highest-leverage next step is an independent trust root for capability issuance, followed by enforceable seat confinement and durable denial receipts. Together these buy three missing properties: proof of who authorized the work, proof that the worker could not exceed that authority, and reconstruction of both accepted and rejected actions.
