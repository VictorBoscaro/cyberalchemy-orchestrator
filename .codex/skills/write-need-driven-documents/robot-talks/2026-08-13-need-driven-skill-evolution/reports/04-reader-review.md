The revision substantially addresses the requested reader experience. Two compact phrases still

## Findings

### R1 - The passage test can reject valid deepening and induce artificial novelty

**Evidence:** The skill asks after every passage what the reader can understand or ask “that they
could not before,” and repeats the requirement as a “new understanding or question” in the final
read (`SKILL.md:34-36,103-105`). Yet the immediately following contribution test correctly admits
precision and trust as legitimate movement (`SKILL.md:35-36`). A passage may make a relation more
credible, precise, or consequential without creating a wholly new understanding or question. The
stricter wording can encourage forced conceptual turns or the deletion of evidence and
reinforcement that actually earn the next passage.

**Minimum correction:** Replace “what the reader can now understand or ask that they could not
before” with “what has changed in what the reader can understand or ask,” and make the same change
in the final-read sentence. This preserves a discriminating state-change test while allowing
deepening rather than requiring novelty.

### R2 - Sequential autonomy is narrowed to advancing a question

**Evidence:** A document in a sequence must “advance a new question” (`SKILL.md:38-40`), while the
skill's stated destination may instead be an understanding, judgment, or decision (`SKILL.md:10-12`)
and its general transition rule permits either understanding or a question (`SKILL.md:34-36`). The
sequence-specific wording can make an essay manufacture an interrogative progression even when its
real contribution is a distinction, judgment, or consequence.

**Minimum correction:** Replace “and advance a new question” with “and use it to advance the
document's own movement.” This keeps the requirement that the document add something beyond recap
without prescribing the form of that addition.

## Refuted concerns

- **The opening rule creates a stock formula:** Refuted. It permits a condition, relation, or tension
  and tests specificity through noun substitution without requiring a scene, anecdote, thesis, or
  visible signpost (`SKILL.md:27-32`). It is specific enough to reject generic openings and open
  enough to preserve voice.
- **The closure rule merely restates the thesis:** Refuted. It explicitly requires the opening
  situation to become newly intelligible or judgeable and rejects route recap (`SKILL.md:103-106`).
  This is a transformation test, not a prescribed closing form.
- **The restructuring instruction encourages sentence polishing:** Refuted. It requires recovery of
  the attempted movement and directs revision from the first structural break rather than polishing
  downstream prose (`SKILL.md:22-25`). The instruction is concise but operational.
- **The skill itself reads mechanically:** Refuted apart from R1-R2. The rules are framed as private
  diagnostic tests, explicitly prohibit exposing a template or checklist, and preserve rhythm,
  warmth, and emphasis when they aid comprehension (`SKILL.md:14-15,68-69`).
