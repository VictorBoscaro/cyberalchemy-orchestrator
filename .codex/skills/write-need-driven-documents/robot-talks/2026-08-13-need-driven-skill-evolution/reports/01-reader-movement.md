# Investigator 01 — Reader movement

## Key Findings

### 1. The opening rule is directionally right but cannot reject a generic opening

The contract says only to begin with something recognizable, then delay distinctions and names
until they are needed (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:22-25`). A broad thesis
or universal observation can satisfy that instruction while still feeling abrupt, interchangeable,
or written from outside the reader's experience. The essay itself begins with a universal claim
about all work and only then supplies an observable human situation
(`plans/governed-agent-work-infrastructure/essays/work-context-system-view/essay.md:21-24`). The
rule therefore describes a desirable quality but does not yet give the writer a discriminating
operation.

The smallest useful addition is an opening test, not an opening formula: establish a perceptible
situation or tension already available to this reader; reject metadiscourse, an unprepared system
thesis, and an opening that could introduce many unrelated essays with only noun substitution. The
opening need not be anecdotal. Its job is to make the next distinction feel earned.

### 2. Necessary passages do not guarantee necessary transitions

The skill asks the writer to name the function of each passage
(`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:27-28`), but several individually useful
paragraphs can still form a sequence of adjacent explanations rather than a movement. Its reorder
test is currently scoped to collections (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:37-39`),
not to paragraphs.

The essay shows the stronger behavior the skill should make repeatable: differentiation makes local
work possible (`plans/governed-agent-work-infrastructure/essays/work-context-system-view/essay.md:43-48`),
local focus changes what remains visible (`plans/governed-agent-work-infrastructure/essays/work-context-system-view/essay.md:74-81`),
and that loss of visibility produces the system need
(`plans/governed-agent-work-infrastructure/essays/work-context-system-view/essay.md:83-88`). A writer
needs a transition test: after each passage, what can the reader now see or ask that they could not
before, and why is the next passage the appropriate response? If deletion or reordering preserves
the apparent movement, the bridge is probably artificial.

### 3. The skill has no rule for a document that is autonomous and sequential

The skill establishes a reader's starting point only at the level of one document
(`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:19-20`). It does not distinguish required local
grounding from recap, or standalone intelligibility from the additional meaning created by order.
That omission is material because the stated Robot-Talks question explicitly requires autonomous
documents that gain meaning in sequence
(`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/robot-talks/2026-08-13-need-driven-skill-evolution/dialogue.md:18-20`).

The behavioral rule should be: reconstruct only the premise needed to enter the present movement,
without mentioning or summarizing the predecessor; then advance a question that is genuinely new.
Two tests are needed. Read alone, the document must supply enough causal ground to understand its
claim. Read after its predecessor, the opening must feel like a continuation in understanding, not
a restart in exposition.

### 4. The ending requirement names correspondence but not closure

The final-read rule says the opening must establish a need that the ending can answer
(`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:94-100`). This permits a summary, a slogan, or a
premature architectural answer, provided it appears responsive. It does not tell the writer what
change should be perceptible at the end.

An operational ending test should ask whether the reader can now understand or judge the opening
situation differently. The ending may name the newly earned concept or expose the next unresolved
tension, but it should not recatalogue the route. The essay's movement from “purpose becomes less
visible” to a proposed responsibility for the system illustrates closure through consequence rather
than recap (`plans/governed-agent-work-infrastructure/essays/work-context-system-view/essay.md:83-88`).

### 5. Material restructuring is routed but not instructed

The description explicitly includes material restructuring
(`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:2-3`), while the body mostly instructs forward
composition. Its revision instruction is only to revise the failing passage
(`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:96-100`). That can encourage sentence-level
repair when the failure is the reader's route itself.

For restructuring, the writer first needs to recover the movement the draft currently attempts:
starting understanding, successive changes, and intended destination. The writer should locate the
first point where a concept arrives unearned, a paragraph fails to change the reader's state, or the
text restarts. Only then should passages be moved or rewritten. This is a distinct operation but
still belongs inside the skill's narrow responsibility.

## Gaps or Inconsistencies

- Voice is protected only by “keep rhythm, warmth, and emphasis”
  (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:56-57`). This is too vague to influence a
  writer, but expanding it into general prose advice would violate the skill's narrow scope. Voice
  guidance should remain tied to reader movement: cadence, repetition, or restraint earns its place
  only when it makes a distinction, transition, or consequence easier to perceive.
- “Something the reader can recognize” (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:22`) and
  “the reader's starting point” (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:19`) are not yet
  connected. Recognition should be relative to the established reader and desired change, not a
  generic appeal to common experience.
- The final read checks premature concepts, hidden relations, claims, and attention cost
  (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:96-100`) but has no falsifiable check for a
  smooth-sounding sequence that does not transform understanding.

## Local Tensions

- **Specific opening guidance versus formulaic openings.** Adding preferred forms such as anecdotes,
  questions, or concrete scenes would merely create a new template. Negative boundaries and an
  interchangeability test provide more constraint with less stylistic capture.
- **Standalone context versus repetition.** Too little reconstruction makes the second document
  dependent; too much makes ordered reading feel punitive. The object to preserve is the minimum
  causal premise, not the predecessor's terminology or section summary.
- **Subtle progression versus explicit signposting.** Requiring every paragraph to announce its
  purpose would damage the prose. The transition test should guide composition and revision while
  remaining invisible in the artifact, consistent with the prohibition on exposing the method
  (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:14-15`).
- **Voice versus cognitive economy.** Rhythm can carry a reader through abstraction, but it can also
  disguise a missing relation. Voice should intensify a load-bearing movement, never substitute for
  one.

## Questions for Synthesis

1. Should the opening rule include one compact negative example of an interchangeable universal
   opening, or would a test alone produce enough behavioral change?
2. Should the paragraph-transition test extend the existing reorder test, or remain separate so
   collections and reader movement are not conflated?
3. What is the shortest wording that captures both halves of sequential autonomy: sufficient local
   grounding and a non-redundant ordered benefit?
4. Should restructuring receive its own short subsection, or a conditional paragraph under
   “Compose the reader's movement” to avoid increasing the skill's visible machinery?
5. Can ending and voice guidance remain operational without adding examples, or are one negative
   ending and one movement-bearing rhythm example necessary for reliable behavior?
