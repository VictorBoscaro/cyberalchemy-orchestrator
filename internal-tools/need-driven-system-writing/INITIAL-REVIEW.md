# Initial Review: Work and Knowledge Overview

## Review mode

This review was performed by direct read-only subagents after the governed runtime rejected the
required connected review topology. It is not a governed dispatch and carries no runtime binding,
registration, or review receipt.

## Converged finding

The original essay did more than introduce terminology too early. It treated one person using AI
agents as the defining composition of the system. That made a particular application appear to be
the system's ontology.

The revised premise is broader and explicitly remains a design intention: the model should
represent work performed by one person, groups of any size, agents, tools, services, or mixed
compositions. AI-assisted software work is the first application, not the definition of the
system.

## Editorial sequence

The reviewers converged on this movement:

1. Begin with work and the difficulty of administering it as composition, dependencies, duration,
   or scale change.
2. Derive the relationships that need to remain explicit: objective, parts, responsibilities,
   decisions, limits, events, results, and evidence.
3. Show those relationships in use before assigning internal names.
4. Introduce names such as `dispatch`, `participant`, `role`, `attempt`, and `authority` only when
   they preserve a distinction already understood.
5. Name `ephemeral work infrastructure` only after its constituent operating responsibilities have
   been presented.
6. Derive `knowledge` from the distinction among stored information, provenance, evidence, review,
   acceptance, and applicability.
7. Name the work and knowledge systems only after each has become independently recognizable.

## Precision risks retained for future reviews

- Treat support for arbitrary work compositions as a requirement, not a demonstrated universal
  capability.
- Keep objective ownership, participation, authorization, and result acceptance as distinct roles.
- Do not treat a record as proof of success, provenance as truth, or acceptance as universal
  validity.
- Distinguish representing a work composition from controlling or correctly understanding it.
- State exactly which operating structures may end and which records must remain.
- Judge manageability through observable conditions rather than participant or agent count.

## Post-rewrite audit

An independent read-only audit of the revised essay found and corrected five remaining conflations:

- a design requirement presented as a universal fact;
- authority attached interchangeably to actors and roles;
- an attempt confused with the records of its execution;
- a work record treated as proof of what occurred;
- acceptance treated as sufficient authorization for later use.

After these corrections, the audit found no other material failure in progression, term timing,
scope, or scientific-business voice.
