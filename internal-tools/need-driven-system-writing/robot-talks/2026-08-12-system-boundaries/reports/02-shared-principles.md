# Key Findings

- **Candidate shared core: a purpose-driven document should declare or make recoverable the change it is meant to produce for a situated reader, and its parts should be judged against that use.** The failure this prevents is local correctness without a successful whole: polished sentences can leave the reader unable to identify the intended change, the logic of the sequence, or the trustworthiness of the conclusion (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:19-33`). The essay defines composition through intended reader effect and gives each part a contribution test (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:35-55`); the skill operationalizes the same obligation as reader starting point, intended transformation, relevance, and boundary (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:8-29`). This is a candidate only for purpose-driven writing, not yet for every textual form.
- **Candidate shared core: relationships among parts must do defensible work at both local and whole-document scales, but the required relation is form-dependent.** The motivating failure is that a precise sentence, coherent paragraph, or strong section can still support the wrong whole or receive the wrong emphasis (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:57-81`). Cumulative prose may require prepared dependencies and meaningful order, while modular/reference writing may require routes, comparisons, and locally sufficient context instead (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:83-101,153-164`). The work-context lineage independently demonstrates the same part/whole failure: tasks can remain locally correct after their purpose or assumptions have changed (`plans/governed-agent-work-infrastructure/essays/work-context-system-view/essay.md:74-88,143-184`). The shared principle is therefore functional and relational composition, not one universal narrative sequence.
- **Candidate shared core: epistemic force must remain within the kind and strength of its support, with uncertainty, proposal status, and limits visible where they qualify reliance.** The failure is manufactured entitlement: a conclusion becomes stronger than its preparation, provenance is mistaken for truth, or acceptance is mistaken for permission or verification. The composition essay names fidelity, logic, semantic scope, and observable evidence as separate safeguards (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:103-124,142-151`); the skill requires explicit separation of existence, proposal, inference, evidence, acceptance, authority, and general validity (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:118-131`). The lineage supplies the deeper justification: different kinds of warrant do not substitute for one another (`docs/essays/what-this-is-for/essay.md:27-57`), and provenance makes later judgment inspectable without itself making a claim true (`plans/governed-agent-work-infrastructure/essays/work-context-system-view/essay.md:301-320`).
- **Most surface prescriptions are preferences or context-specific criteria, not shared principles.** A fixed situation-to-proposal progression, terminology only after practical need, concrete-before-abstract diction, short sections, self-explanatory headings, visible actors, cadence, warmth, and musical vocabulary may serve the current external-explanation objective, but the sources themselves qualify them: the sequence is a default rather than a mandatory table of contents and reference documents may be modular (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:31-53`); shortness and abstraction rules admit exceptions (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:55-71,95-116`); and the musical model is explicitly exploratory (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:278-305`). The essay states directly that criteria vary with document, audience, and purpose and are not universal laws (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:204-223`). A shared core should constrain failures; profiles and form-specific methods should choose expression.

# Gaps or Inconsistencies

- The composition essay is a draft and explicitly says its categories are neither complete, independent, nor empirically validated; its inventory and flow remain proposals to test (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:14-17,323-333`). The cited essays share repository lineage, so agreement among them is not independent confirmation.
- The evidence does not yet establish the requested universality across all document forms. The essay's primary definition is limited to purpose-driven prose read substantially in sequence (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:35-46`), then adds modular/reference exceptions. Fiction, ritual, expressive writing, contracts, dictionaries, and other forms are named only as reasons criteria differ, not studied cases (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:217-219`).
- The ontology's universal orthogonality claim is internally too strong for use as a shared writing principle. It requires statistical independence and “zero redundancy” (`vault/ontology-conventions.md:24-45,323-340`), yet later concedes that `nature` has lower independent entropy and correlates with `node_type` while remaining useful (`vault/ontology-conventions.md:192-201`). The defensible transferable lesson is to keep materially different judgments distinct, not to require zero mutual information.
- No source supplies a validated admission test that cleanly classifies a proposed rule as shared core, form convention, personal preference, or merely one useful technique. The distinction among dimension, lens, criterion, and evidence helps locate disagreement, but is itself new synthesis (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:111-124,323-327`).

# Local Tensions

- The README and skill foreground a need-driven linear progression and terminology-after-referent discipline (`internal-tools/need-driven-system-writing/README.md:18-45`; `.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:31-53,73-81`), while the essay says modular and reference forms compose through navigation and local sufficiency (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:88-92`). Treating the former as universal would erase a distinction the conceptual source preserves.
- “Every part makes a necessary contribution” can become an over-strict deletion rule. The same sources allow recurrence, pacing, emphasis, reading pleasure, and useful return, whose contribution may be experiential rather than logically indispensable (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:48-55,94-101,176-184`; `.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:159-166,194-200`). “Contribution can be named and justified” is better supported than “removal must break the argument.”
- The ontology treats document `nature` as a reading instruction with four structural formats (`vault/ontology-conventions.md:182-205,420-427`), but the essay recognizes scanning, lookup, rereading, and branching navigation as still-open compositional cases (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:307-321`). The existing format vocabulary should inform contextual criteria, not bound the shared theory prematurely.
- “Assume intelligence, not prior vocabulary” is strong guidance for the tool's initial external-system-explanation target (`internal-tools/need-driven-system-writing/README.md:3-16,34-45`), but it is not evidence of a principle across all audiences and forms. The more portable obligation is to make reader assumptions explicit and fit them to purpose.

# Questions for Synthesis

- Is the shared core explicitly scoped to purpose-driven, non-fictional documents, or must it govern every textual form? The evidence supports the former and leaves the latter open.
- Should the core be stated as failure-preventing constraints—purpose/use is recoverable, support licenses claim strength, part/whole relations are defensible, and review judgments cite evidence—rather than positive style rules?
- What evidence would promote a candidate from repository practice to shared principle: cross-form counterexamples, reader studies, inter-reviewer agreement, or demonstrated revision benefit?
- Should editorial economy remain folded into functional contribution, or does it deserve its own core principle despite legitimate repetition, redundancy, ceremony, and aesthetic surplus?
- How will synthesis prevent profile preferences from silently becoming universal criteria while still allowing a profile to choose strong defaults for a particular author and document family?
