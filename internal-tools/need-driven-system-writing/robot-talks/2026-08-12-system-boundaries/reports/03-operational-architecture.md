## Key Findings

- The smallest coherent architecture is an ownership chain, not a component stack: **conceptual model → author profile → document brief → composition procedure → independent review → observed-use residue → explicit promotion**. The conceptual essay already limits itself to a draft model and identifies its inventory and evaluation flow as proposals to test; the operational skill turns reader transformation and claim status into a procedure. Keeping those as separate owners prevents a revisable theory from becoming an implicit runtime rule (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:14-17, 323-327`; `.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:17-29`).

- Specialization should occur only at a distinct contract boundary. The general editorial procedure explicitly composes with artifact-specific skills while leaving their formal contracts with them, and `system-view` demonstrates that boundary by owning shape and stance naming while deferring definitions, verdicts, schemas, and mechanics. Thus a specialization should add a document form, required fields, or gate that the composition core does not own—not restate general prose guidance (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:14-15`; `.agents/skills/system-view/SKILL.md:29-40, 63-79`).

- Schemas earn a place at handoffs, not as a complete representation of writing quality. Whisper's useful structural pattern is explicit state ownership plus artifact-specific gates, while the interview skill makes modes pluggable through applicability, allowed targets, exit criteria, and verdict rules. The evaluation model, however, says criteria are document-specific and should not become mechanical. The minimum is therefore a small profile contract, a per-document brief/transport contract, and evidence-bearing review findings—not one schema for judgment itself (`.agents/skills/whisper/SKILL.md:57-70, 72-82`; `.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md:62-74`; `vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:204-223, 260-271`).

- Writing and review should share vocabulary but remain different actions. Composition may include an author's finish check, but independent review targets an existing artifact, stays read-only, and produces verified, cited change requests rather than edits. This preserves author/profile ownership while allowing specialized lenses without creating parallel writing authorities (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:168-188, 190-202`; `.agents/skills/review/SKILL.md:8-13, 125-145`).

- Learning from use requires a one-way evidence boundary: runs may append observations, but only the owner of a profile, principle, or skill may promote them. Whisper already distinguishes learning residue from canonical author voice or transport rules and defers durable promotion; the vault model separately treats validation as evidence and requires real-use review for maturity. This is enough for learning without an adaptive system silently rewriting its own authority (`.agents/skills/whisper/SKILL.md:80-84, 105-114`; `vault/ontology-conventions.md:315-319, 429-445`).

## Gaps or Inconsistencies

- No bounded source defines a personal writing-profile artifact, its canonical location, its minimum fields, or who may amend it. Whisper contains tone, voice, register, audience, and trajectory fields, but owns them as run state rather than as a person-owned durable profile (`.agents/skills/whisper/SKILL.md:57-66, 116-126`).

- There is no explicit precedence rule for conflicts among shared principles, personal preferences, document/transport requirements, and evidence constraints. Without it, “profile” can become either decorative or an authority capable of overriding fidelity.

- The current installed ownership metadata is not operable as written: Whisper declares `spells/whisper/README.md` as canonical and Interrogation declares `arcana/structured-interview-kits/SKILL.md`, but neither path exists at the repository root inspected in this run (`.agents/skills/whisper/SKILL.md:4-9`; `.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md:2-7, 32-37`). Generated copies therefore identify an owner that a maintainer cannot reach through the declared path.

- The internal-tool README says the folder contains only objectives and initial principles, while the actual procedural skill and draft theory live elsewhere; it gives no navigation or promotion path connecting those owners (`internal-tools/need-driven-system-writing/README.md:49-58`; `vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:323-360`).

- Research questions are listed, but no bounded artifact assigns their intake, evidence format, decision gate, or promotion owner (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:307-327`).

## Local Tensions

- Whisper currently claims intake, schema selection, planning, drafting, review, validation, and learning under one lifecycle. That is coherent as a product workflow, but it overlaps the separately owned composition, interview, and review capabilities and risks making Whisper a second canonical home for each (`.agents/skills/whisper/SKILL.md:21-25, 68-84, 105-114`).

- `write-need-driven-documents` contains a substantial review checklist, while `review` reserves independent red-team review for existing artifacts. The sources do not name the boundary between authorial self-check and independent evaluative review, so “review” can trigger two materially different contracts (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:168-202`; `.agents/skills/review/SKILL.md:8-13, 48-79`).

- The proposed theory separates dimensions, lenses, criteria, and evidence, yet the writing skill embeds a fixed dimension table. That table is useful as a seed, but treating it as canonical now would outrun the essay's explicit draft and unvalidated status (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:111-124, 309-327`; `.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:168-188`).

- The vault's anti-duplication rule is operationally useful at the node level, but its stronger claim that all labels should have zero mutual information conflicts with its admission that `nature` is correlated with `node_type`. Architecture should use the removable-information test, not pretend statistical independence has been demonstrated (`vault/ontology-conventions.md:24-45, 197-201, 323-350`).

## Questions for Synthesis

- Is `write-need-driven-documents` the proposed composition core, or a system-explanation specialization from which a smaller core still needs to be extracted?
- Should Whisper remain an end-to-end product workflow that composes canonical capabilities, or does it own any of composition, profile, review, and learning semantics itself?
- What is the explicit precedence order when evidence constraints, shared principles, a person's profile, and a document/transport contract disagree?
- Which profile changes require direct author confirmation, and can observed residue ever promote automatically?
- Who owns conversion of research results into candidate principles or skill changes, and what evidence gate prevents one successful document from becoming a general rule?
