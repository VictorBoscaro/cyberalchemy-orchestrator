# Finding Review

## Finding 1 — AMEND

Whisper does show that one composition intake can span resonance, audience/domain relevance, and trajectory rather than tone alone (`.agents/skills/whisper/SKILL.md:116-126`). It does not establish that this is the “most complete” model, that every listed field belongs in an author profile, or that per-document fields such as transport, objective, opening, ending, and length describe durable author identity. Its proof target and L0 optimization are transport-specific (`.agents/skills/whisper/SKILL.md:21-25,51-55`).

**Exact amended claim:** Whisper provides one candidate per-text intake model that extends beyond tone across felt effect, reader/domain fit, and intended movement. It supports asking about desired effect, relationship to the reader, structural movement, and aversions, but does not establish that all of its fields are required or stable components of an author-owned writing profile.

## Finding 2 — AMEND

Interrogation requires a cheap evidence baseline, one highest-discrimination question at a time, explicit uncertainty where evidence is absent, and no invented user answers (`.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md:39-59,76-96`). It does not define existing writing, revision behavior, or concrete choices as the evidence baseline, nor has it been validated as a preference-discovery method.

**Exact amended claim:** If Interrogation is adapted for writing-preference discovery, its supported procedural defaults are to inspect available evidence first, ask one high-discrimination question at a time, and preserve explicit uncertainty rather than invent answers. The bounded sources do not specify which writing evidence should be inspected or show that this process is sufficient to discover an author’s preferences.

## Finding 3 — AMEND

The sources support partial traceability: Interrogation records selected and rejected alternatives and updates the target artifact after an answer (`.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md:55-59`); Whisper records user corrections and requires learning residue to remain distinct from canonical author voice or truth (`.agents/skills/whisper/SKILL.md:79-83,143-156`). They do not require a durable correction history, distinguish explicit statement from inference and later validation, or define author acceptance, rejection, promotion, and revocation of preferences. Immediate patching could overwrite rather than preserve history.

**Exact amended claim:** Existing practices partially support author ownership by tracing decisions and user corrections and by preventing learning residue from automatically becoming canonical author voice. They do not yet define a durable correction history, explicit-versus-inferred preference states, or an author-ratification lifecycle; inferred preferences therefore cannot be treated as canonical on the authority of these sources alone.

## Finding 4 — AMEND

The README and writing skill do contain strong bounded defaults: progressive disclosure, plain naming, visible actors, economy, and phrasing subordinate to the idea (`internal-tools/need-driven-system-writing/README.md:3-16,34-45`; `.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:95-116`). The essay explicitly makes criteria dependent on document, audience, and purpose and rejects mechanical universalization (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:204-223`). This supports keeping house defaults distinguishable from author preferences. The sources do not themselves prove that the return must use the investigator’s proposed three-way classification or specify its exact representation.

**Exact amended claim:** The current editorial method contains strong defaults suited to a bounded document class, while the composition essay treats adequacy criteria as context-dependent. A profile interview should therefore keep author-supplied preferences distinguishable from task/form constraints and method defaults; the bounded sources do not yet specify the exact return format needed to preserve that distinction.

# Gaps or Inconsistencies Review

- **Writing-profile interview mode — source-grounded and useful.** Interrogation defines only the extension contract for a mode (`.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md:62-74`), and Whisper invokes generic structured interviewing for ambiguities rather than defining an author-profile mode (`.agents/skills/whisper/SKILL.md:34-40,105-114`).
- **Insufficient generic result contract — source-grounded and useful.** The output reports scope, mode, counts, ambiguities, verdict, and next step, but none of the author-ownership evidence named by the investigator (`.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md:99-113`).
- **No canonical-preference lifecycle — source-grounded with one omitted qualification.** Whisper does defer durable knowledge to inventory or glossary owners (`.agents/skills/whisper/SKILL.md:105-114`), so it has a generic promotion route. The report is still correct that it supplies no author-facing rule for promoting, correcting, or revoking canonical voice preferences.
- **No required comparison with samples or editing behavior — source-grounded and useful.** The evidence baseline is unspecified (`.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md:39-47,76-85`); neither cited source makes author-produced text or observed revisions mandatory.
- **Initial status cannot support cross-author efficacy — source-grounded and useful.** The tool records only an objective and initial principles and is not a finished method or validator (`internal-tools/need-driven-system-writing/README.md:47-58`). The stronger conclusion about efficacy across authors is a warranted limitation, not a reported test result.

# Local Tensions Review

- **Recommended defaults versus author anchoring — useful hypothesis, not an established source tension.** Interrogation does require a recommended default when safe (`.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md:47-54`), but the sources contain no evidence that authors anchor on interviewer vocabulary. Synthesis should preserve this as a risk to test, not a finding.
- **Immediate patching versus premature crystallization — partly grounded, partly hypothetical.** Immediate patching and decision recording are explicit (`.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md:55-59`), and no reconciliation or ratification pass is specified. The claim that early answers will crystallize is plausible but untested.
- **Transport-specific learning versus durable identity — source-grounded and useful.** Whisper’s first proof target and L0 optimization are explicitly Substack-specific while learning residue feeds future runs (`.agents/skills/whisper/SKILL.md:21-25,51-66,128-140`). The risk of scope leakage is directly relevant to author sovereignty.
- **Bounded editorial defaults versus neutral profile language — source-grounded and useful.** The writing skill’s defaults are real and bounded to a stated document class (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:1-3,55-71,95-116`). Treating them as neutral descriptors would erase provenance, although that misuse remains a prospective risk rather than observed behavior.
- **Structured disagreement versus readiness verdict — overstated as a conflict.** The essay preserves disagreement (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:186-202,229-258`), but Interrogation’s verdict explicitly includes remaining ambiguities and distinguishes blocker from non-blocker ambiguity (`.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md:58-59,76-85,99-113`). A verdict need not close a stylistic tension. Synthesis should ask whether a profile mode needs a richer disposition vocabulary, not treat the current contracts as contradictory.
