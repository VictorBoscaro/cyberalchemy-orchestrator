# Author Sovereignty and the Interview Layer

## Key Findings

- A genuine writing profile must describe more than tone. The most complete existing intake model spans felt meaning (`tone`, `voice`, `style register`, `emotional residue`, `value signal`, and `forbidden feels`), reader and domain fit (`target public`, `reader state`, `authority mode`, `assumptions`, and `objections`), and intended movement (`objective`, form, anchor, structure, opening, ending, and length). An interviewer should therefore elicit the author's desired effect, relationship to the reader, characteristic movement, and aversions—not reduce identity to a handful of stylistic adjectives. (`.agents/skills/whisper/SKILL.md`, lines 116–126)

- Preference discovery should begin from evidence and use one discriminating question at a time. `interrogation` requires a cheap evidence baseline, chooses the question most likely to resolve a blocker or expose risk, and asks exactly one question with context and consequences; it explicitly rejects broad generic questions, question-bank dumps, and invented answers. This supports an interview that observes existing writing, stated uncertainty, and concrete choices before asking the author to theorize about their own style. (`.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md`, lines 39–59, 76–96)

- Author ownership requires a visible correction history and a strict distinction between what the person said, what the system inferred, and what later use appeared to validate. Existing practices record selected and rejected alternatives with rationale, update artifacts when answers change them, collect user-correction signals, and require reusable residue to remain distinct from canonical author voice or transport rules. Those controls imply that inferred preferences are provisional until the author accepts, revises, or rejects them. (`.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md`, lines 55–59; `.agents/skills/whisper/SKILL.md`, lines 79–83, 143–156)

- The interviewer must not present the current editorial method as if it were the author's discovered voice. The internal tool and writing skill already favor directness, progressive disclosure, plain naming, visible actors, economy, and prose whose phrasing does not eclipse the idea; meanwhile, the composition essay says adequacy criteria depend on the document, audience, and purpose and warns against mechanical criteria. Personalization is genuine only if the return identifies which constraints came from the task, which defaults came from the house method, and which preferences came from the author. (`internal-tools/need-driven-system-writing/README.md`, lines 3–16, 34–45; `.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md`, lines 95–116; `vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md`, lines 204–223)

## Gaps or Inconsistencies

- No bounded source defines an interview mode specifically for discovering an author's writing profile. `interrogation` supplies the extension contract—applicability signals, question formation, patch targets, exit criteria, and verdict rules—but not the relevant mode itself. (`.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md`, lines 62–74)

- The generic interview result reports counts, ambiguities, and a readiness verdict, but it does not require the author's own wording, cited examples, inferred-versus-explicit status, confidence, rejected characterizations, or affirmative sign-off. That output is adequate for decision readiness but insufficient evidence that a writing identity remains author-owned. (`.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md`, lines 99–113)

- The sources name user corrections and learning residue, but they do not say how an observed pattern becomes—or ceases to be—a canonical preference. Whisper explicitly distinguishes residue from canonical voice, yet provides no author-facing promotion, correction, or revocation rule. (`.agents/skills/whisper/SKILL.md`, lines 79–83, 143–156)

- There is no explicit requirement to compare self-description with writing samples, revision choices, or reactions to alternatives. The interview process asks for an evidence baseline, but it never defines author-produced text or observed editing behavior as necessary evidence for a profile. (`.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md`, lines 39–47, 76–85)

- The internal tool is still only an objective and initial principles, not a finished editorial method, so its current preferences cannot yet substantiate claims about what works across authors. (`internal-tools/need-driven-system-writing/README.md`, lines 47–58)

## Local Tensions

- `Interrogation` asks the interviewer to offer a recommended default when safe, yet author-profile discovery is unusually vulnerable to anchoring: the author may endorse the interviewer's vocabulary rather than recognize their own preference. (`.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md`, lines 47–54)

- Immediate artifact updates preserve traceability, but they may crystallize an early answer before later examples reveal contradiction, context dependence, or a better formulation. The process specifies immediate patching and decision recording without a later profile-level reconciliation or author ratification pass. (`.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md`, lines 55–59)

- Whisper intends to preserve author intent, but its first proof target is a Substack research post and its L0 operator accepts optimization for that transport. Without an explicit boundary, lessons about one form, audience, or draft can be mistaken for durable author identity. (`.agents/skills/whisper/SKILL.md`, lines 21–25, 51–55, 128–140)

- The writing skill preserves “rhythm” and “personality” while also preferring simple wording, visible actors, clear headings, short sections, and phrasing less noticeable than the idea. These may be useful defaults for its stated document class, but an interviewer using them as neutral descriptors would quietly convert a bounded house method into the author's profile. (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md`, lines 55–71, 95–116)

- The composition model recognizes that lenses create structured disagreement and that review can uncover disagreement about purpose itself, but the interview contract culminates in `pass`, `flag`, or `block`. A readiness verdict can close operational ambiguity while leaving a legitimate, author-owned stylistic tension unresolved. (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md`, lines 186–202, 229–258; `.agents/skills/interrogation/SKILL.md`, lines 58–59)

## Questions for Synthesis

- What evidence should be sufficient to call a preference explicit, inferred, observed, context-bound, or author-ratified?

- How should the synthesis prevent the interviewer's recommended defaults and the current need-driven method from becoming invisible answers to the questions being asked?

- What must be returned to the author so they can recognize themselves, inspect provenance, preserve contradictions, correct misreadings, and revoke earlier preferences without losing history?

- Which preferences should remain deliberately conditional on audience, purpose, document form, or moment rather than being consolidated into a stable identity claim?

- What kind of comparison—examples, alternatives, revision behavior, or reader outcomes—would test a person's self-description without allowing observed behavior to overrule their stated ownership?
