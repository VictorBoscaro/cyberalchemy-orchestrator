# Independent Review — Investigator 02: Shared Principles and Composition

## Numbered Findings

### Finding 1 — ACCEPT

The claim is explicitly limited to purpose-driven writing and presented as a candidate, so it does not overstate universality. The cited failure is literal: locally polished parts can leave purpose, sequence, and warranted conclusion unclear, while the essay and skill both orient composition around a defined reader change and the function of parts (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:19-55`; `.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:8-29`). “Declare or make recoverable” is a defensible synthesis: the skill requires the transformation to be stated before drafting, while the essay requires it to govern the document without requiring an explicit declaration inside the prose.

### Finding 2 — AMEND

The part/whole failure and form-dependent distinction are supported (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:57-101,153-164`). However, the work-context essay does not “independently demonstrate” the principle: the composition essay explicitly names that essay as a source of its part-to-whole movement (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:353-360`). The claim also needs the same purpose-driven scope as the essay; the cited work-context analogy extends provenance, not cross-form validation.

**Amended claim:** For purpose-driven prose, relationships among parts should make a defensible contribution at both local and whole-document scales, while the relation required depends on form: cumulative prose may rely on prepared sequence, whereas modular or reference prose may rely on navigable routes, comparisons, dependencies, and locally sufficient context. This is a candidate beyond those forms, not a demonstrated universal.

### Finding 3 — AMEND

The sources strongly support separating claim strength from evidence, provenance, acceptance, and permission, and they identify substitution among kinds of warrant as a concrete failure (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:103-151`; `.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:118-131`; `docs/essays/what-this-is-for/essay.md:27-57`; `plans/governed-agent-work-infrastructure/essays/work-context-system-view/essay.md:301-320`). But the report states the obligation without an applicability condition. Fictional, ritual, expressive, and other non-assertoric passages do not necessarily ask to be relied on as claims. The two lineage essays are also draft, proposal-only sources, so they deepen the rationale without validating universality (`docs/essays/what-this-is-for/essay.md:1-25`; `plans/governed-agent-work-infrastructure/essays/work-context-system-view/essay.md:1-15`).

**Amended claim:** Wherever a text asks a reader to rely on a factual, inferential, or normative claim, the claim's force should remain within the kind and strength of its support, and uncertainty, proposal status, provenance limits, and reliance limits should remain visible where they matter.

### Finding 4 — ACCEPT

The finding correctly distinguishes shared failure constraints from stylistic defaults. The skill qualifies its sequence as a default, gives modular reference documents an exception, and makes shortness and abstraction context-sensitive; the essay explicitly denies that context-specific criteria are universal laws and treats the musical vocabulary as exploratory (`.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:31-53,55-71,95-116`; `vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:204-223,278-305`). “Preferences or context-specific criteria” is appropriately disjunctive: not every listed prescription is personal taste, but none is established here as universal.

The final allocation to profiles and form-specific methods approaches the operational-architecture boundary, but it does not design an interview, storage model, tool, or implementation. It remains a legitimate boundary statement about what the shared core should not own.

## Local Tensions

1. **AMEND.** There is no contradiction within the sources' stated scopes: the README addresses external system explanations, and the skill itself permits modular reference structure. The tension appears only if their need-driven linear default is promoted into the shared core. **Amended tension:** Scoped linear guidance for external explanations could be misclassified as universal despite the conceptual source's form-dependent account (`internal-tools/need-driven-system-writing/README.md:3-45`; `.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:31-53`; `vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:88-92`).
2. **AMEND.** The sources do not actually equate contribution with logical indispensability: they name reinforcement, retrieval, pacing, emphasis, pleasure, and useful return as valid functions. **Amended tension:** The word “necessary” and the deletion test invite an over-strict reading unless the system preserves the broader standard that a contribution may be experiential, orienting, or emphatic and only needs to be nameable and justified (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:48-55,94-101,176-184,260-276`; `.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:159-166,194-200`).
3. **REJECT.** The ontology's `nature` values classify document shape as a reading instruction; the essay's linear, scanning, lookup, rereading, and branching cases describe traversal modes and open compositional behavior. These are different axes, so the cited passages do not establish a local contradiction or premature bound (`vault/ontology-conventions.md:182-205,420-427`; `vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:307-321`).
4. **AMEND.** “Assume intelligence, not prior vocabulary” is supported guidance for the initial external-explanation target, not evidence of universality. **Amended tension:** The scoped guidance could become a disguised universal reader model if synthesis fails to reduce it to the more portable candidate obligation to make reader assumptions explicit and fit them to purpose (`internal-tools/need-driven-system-writing/README.md:3-16,34-45`; `.codex/skills/write-need-driven-documents/SKILL.md:17-29,73-81`).

## Questions for Synthesis

1. **RETAIN.** Scope must be decided before any candidate is called shared; the evidence presently supports purpose-driven nonfiction more strongly than all text.
2. **RETAIN WITH CAUTION.** Failure-preventing constraints are better supported than positive style rules, but “review judgments cite evidence” should remain a review principle unless synthesis establishes why it constrains composition itself.
3. **RETAIN.** The sources explicitly leave empirical benefit, cross-form fit, reader response, and inter-reviewer consistency open (`vault/essays/evaluating-text-as-composition.md:307-327`).
4. **RETAIN.** Editorial economy is not yet demonstrated as universal, and the cited sources recognize legitimate recurrence, emphasis, pacing, and return.
5. **RETAIN.** Preventing profile defaults from becoming universal criteria is central to the shared-principles boundary; the question does not prescribe interview flow, storage, or implementation.

## Eligible for synthesis

1. For purpose-driven writing, a document should declare or make recoverable the change it is meant to produce for a situated reader, and its parts should be judged against that use.
2. For purpose-driven prose, relationships among parts should make a defensible contribution at both local and whole-document scales, while the relation required depends on form: cumulative prose may rely on prepared sequence, whereas modular or reference prose may rely on navigable routes, comparisons, dependencies, and locally sufficient context. This is a candidate beyond those forms, not a demonstrated universal.
3. Wherever a text asks a reader to rely on a factual, inferential, or normative claim, the claim's force should remain within the kind and strength of its support, and uncertainty, proposal status, provenance limits, and reliance limits should remain visible where they matter.
4. Most surface prescriptions in the bounded sources are preferences or context-specific criteria, not shared principles. A shared core should constrain demonstrated failures; profiles and form-specific methods may choose expression.
