# Advisor brief — formal and structural owner map

## Operational question

How do specific formal traditions represent the formation of a whole from parts, and what does each tradition require, preserve, or reject when composition occurs?

## Evidence scope

- Use 5–7 primary or official sources.
- Cover at least four distinct formal traditions.
- Treat each tradition on its own terms and within its documented scope.

## Required fields

For every tradition, record:

- `owner`
- `composed_object`
- `operation_or_interface`
- `formation_conditions`
- `laws_or_preservation`
- `failure_or_non_example`
- `scope_and_limits`
- `transfer_risk`

## Evidence discipline

Keep these categories visibly separate:

- **Evidence:** claims directly supported by a cited primary or official source.
- **Paraphrase:** a faithful restatement of a source, cited locally.
- **Inference:** the researcher's conclusion, explicitly marked and tied to the evidence that licenses it.

Do not present an inference as the source's own claim.

## Exclusions

- Do not use the internal repository research as evidence or as a category source.
- Do not universalize a tradition's account of composition.
- Do not perform cross-domain synthesis.
- Do not select or recommend a winning tradition.

## Completion criteria

The run is complete only when:

1. 5–7 primary or official sources have been cited and used substantively.
2. At least four distinct traditions are represented.
3. Every tradition has all eight required fields.
4. Evidence, paraphrase, and inference are explicitly distinguishable.
5. At least one failure or non-example and one transfer risk are stated for every tradition.
6. The exclusions above have been respected.
7. `findings.md` exists.
8. An independent `review.md` exists and contains an explicit literal `PASS` or `BLOCK` verdict.

## Blockers

- `review.md` is absent or lacks an explicit literal `PASS`/`BLOCK` verdict.
- `findings.md` is absent.
- Fewer than 5 primary or official sources, more than 7 sources, or fewer than four traditions are used.
- Any required field is missing.
- Evidence, paraphrase, and inference are conflated.
- Internal research, universalization, cross-domain synthesis, or winner selection enters the result.
