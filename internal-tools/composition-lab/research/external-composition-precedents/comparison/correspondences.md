# Candidate correspondences: formal structures and engineered systems

## Boundary

This matrix compares only the two accepted owner maps. It records candidate correspondences; it does not identify a shared mechanism, define composition generally, or transfer a theorem from a formal owner to an engineered practice. “Collapse fact” names evidence that would defeat the proposed correspondence rather than merely weaken it.

Source keys:

- **F1–F6:** [formal and structural owner map](../runs/formal-structural-owner-map/findings.md), sections 1–6.
- **E1–E5:** [engineered-systems owner map](../runs/engineered-systems-owner-map/findings.md), sections A1–A5.
- Acceptance status: [formal review](../runs/formal-structural-owner-map/review.md) and [engineered-systems review](../runs/engineered-systems-owner-map/review.md).

## Bounded observations

1. The strongest candidate recurrence is not a shared operation but **explicit admissibility**: boundaries, types, requirements, contracts, or environmental conditions determine whether a proposed combination is licensed.
2. The engineered findings repeatedly add obligations absent from the nearest simple formal analogy: environment state, lifecycle validation, readability scope, cardinality, policy, and verification.
3. Formal associativity, identity, closure, and preservation results have no demonstrated engineered counterpart in these inputs. Those remain testable gaps, not inherited properties.
4. “Emergence” has no stable cross-map correspondence. The formal map mostly derives or preserves whole behavior; the NASA account monitors adverse interaction effects. Treating those as one concept would currently be vocabulary transfer only.
5. Decomposition is under-owned in both maps. Nesting, encapsulation, useful subsets, and splitting occur, but none of the paired findings establishes a general inverse, recoverability rule, or residue measure.

No row warrants **same structure** on the present evidence. That label would require an explicit structure-preserving mapping or a demonstrated instantiation of the formal account, neither of which appears in the accepted owner maps.
