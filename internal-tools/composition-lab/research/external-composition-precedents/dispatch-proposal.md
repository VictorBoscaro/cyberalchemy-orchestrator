# Dispatch proposal — external composition precedents

## Question

How do materially different external traditions treat composition, distinguish it from neighboring
phenomena, and delimit which distinctions could legitimately inform composition of skills,
interfaces, artifacts, knowledge, work, agents, and lenses in this project?

## Recommended shape

Four explorers collect independently across formal/structural, parts-wholes/systems,
engineered, and epistemic/social/creative perspectives. A single writer then creates the verbatim
`research.md` collection and cited comparative `findings.md`. Three skeptics independently apply
the canonical precedent, non-vacuity, and definitional-soundness gates. A dedicated auditor checks
the verdict matrix, citations, transfer limits, and final answer.

The research must compare discriminating contrasts, not produce a catalogue or choose a universal
definition. A candidate is a potentially transferable distinction or mechanism, not an entire
field or theory. Ownership labels use; it never kills a candidate. Only no-witness and tautological
collapse produce KILL.

## Corpus policy

Primary papers and books, standards, specifications, institutional publications, and official
technical documentation take precedence. Reviews and handbooks may navigate a field. Each explorer
uses a bounded anchor set, preserves stable bibliographic identity and search decisions, and
includes at least one source exposing a boundary or failure. Unsourced summaries, vendor thought
pieces, dictionary definitions, and analogy-only uses are excluded.

## Outputs

- `research.md`: collected explorer returns, preserved with source boundaries;
- `findings.md`: cited comparative synthesis and the canonical candidate verdict matrix.

Both belong in this folder. No source-tree, product, architecture, runtime, or governance change is
authorized by the research.
