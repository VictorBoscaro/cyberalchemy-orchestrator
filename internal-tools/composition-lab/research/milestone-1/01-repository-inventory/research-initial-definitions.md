# Research Initial Definitions: Repository Lens-Composition Inventory

> **Program status:** preserved as Case 1, the lens-composition pilot. This inquiry supplies one
> empirical corpus to the broader Composition Lab program; it does not define composition in
> general or determine the program's later cases.

## Context

Cyberalchemy Orchestrator develops infrastructure intended to keep agent work connected to the
objectives, decisions, assumptions, actions, and evidence that give it meaning. Composition Lab
separates the investigation of composition from later product or architecture decisions so that
existing practices can be understood without treating the current orchestrator as their necessary
form.

The repository already configures distinguishable perspectives in multi-agent work and contains
prescriptions that treat their combination as useful. That practice matters to the project's
broader purpose because the meaning and warranted use of a combined result depend on knowing what
each perspective contributed, how perspectives were related, and what evidence survives about that
relationship. The present understanding does not yet establish those distinctions consistently.

## Purpose

This document establishes the informational starting point for understanding how distinguishable
perspectives are represented and related in the repository's existing agent practices. That
understanding will inform later decisions about a provisional vocabulary and model for lens
composition, without itself establishing either one.

## Research Question (Can be refined)

How does the repository declare, instantiate, enact, relate, and preserve distinguishable
perspectives directed at a common target, and what existing evidence warrants claims about those
relationships without overstating compositional effect?

## Confirmed Product Constraints

- The immediate subject is the repository's existing use of lenses and distinguishable
  perspectives in agent work; composition in other domains is not established by this inquiry.
  ([Integrated Milestone 1 program](../../../orchestration/milestone-1-strategy/04-integrated-program.md))
- This inquiry does not authorize a canonical definition of lens or composition, a causal theory,
  a product interface, a schema, a runtime, a migration, or extraction to another system.
  ([Composition Lab README](../../../README.md);
  [Integrated Milestone 1 program](../../../orchestration/milestone-1-strategy/04-integrated-program.md))
- The inquiry remains subordinate to Composition Lab's broader investigation of composition rather
  than defining that investigation through the repository's current orchestration architecture.
  ([Composition Lab README](../../../README.md))
- Findings from this pilot require comparison with at least one non-lens domain before they can be
  treated as candidates for a cross-domain account of composition.

## Current Evidence Baseline

- Composition Lab records that the repository already employs composition in multiple senses, but
  that these usages do not demonstrate a shared structure or a general theory.
  ([Composition Lab README](../../../README.md))
- The broader initial definitions state that the project does not yet have an established
  observational vocabulary for how a composition forms, changes, succeeds, fails, or decomposes,
  nor an empirical basis for attributing outcomes to a composition instead of its parts.
  ([Composition research initial definitions](../../research-initial-definitions.md))

## Known Gaps

- It is not known which repository occurrences realize a distinguishable perspective and which
  merely use adjacent labels or terminology.
- The boundaries among a lens, `concern`, `angle`, role, persona, method, prompt, and division of
  work remain unresolved in actual repository practice.
- It is unclear where the repository contains evidence only of prescription or configuration and
  where it preserves evidence of enactment, interaction, transformation, or effect.
- It is not known when multiple perspectives are genuinely related rather than merely parallel,
  sequential, selected, concatenated, or summarized after the fact.
- The extent to which order, information access, independence, feedback, and synthesis are visible
  in preserved traces is unknown.
- It is unclear what contributions, tensions, losses, dependencies, or residues survive in combined
  results and what disappears during synthesis or closure.
- Historical schema changes and multiple records of the same activity may obscure the identity of
  an occurrence and the interpretation of its evidence.
- The repository's preserved evidence may support only limited claims about how lenses are used;
  whether it supports claims about their effects remains unknown.
- It is not known which aspects of repository lens composition, if any, can later be carried into
  other tools, work practices, interfaces, or knowledge systems.
