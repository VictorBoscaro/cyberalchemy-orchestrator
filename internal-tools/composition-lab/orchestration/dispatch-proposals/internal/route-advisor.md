# Route advice — internal composition study

## Verdict

**Operational BLOCK now.** The intended route is an Inventory-owned bounded and unregistered
bootstrap, but the installed Inventory capability does not yet define that delegated workflow and
its declared canonical source, `arcana/inventory/SKILL.md`, is absent from this checkout. This
document does not create, register, authorize, or launch a dispatch.

Do not route the descriptive acquisition stage through registered `research`. Research requires
candidate adjudication through precedent, witness, definitional soundness, verdict, and use-mode.
Turning source occurrences into candidates merely to satisfy that contract would change the
question and inflate the evidence. The established contract analysis reaches the same conclusion
([Research adjudication](../../milestone-1-strategy/d1-readiness/runtime-blocker/08-research-contract-adjudication.md)).

## Why Inventory is the intended owner

The first internal stage asks what the repository contains: source-backed occurrences, explicit
absences, ambiguity, contradictions, relations, traces, and evidence levels. Inventory owns
source-linked evidence-cards, human and machine indexes, trace, residue, validation, and
non-authority handoffs. It does not own definitions, causal conclusions, novelty, or `GO/KILL`.
That boundary matches the accepted [internal initial definitions](../../../research/internal-composition-uses/research-initial-definitions.md)
and the broader [research program](../../../research-program.md).

The repository already has `.arcanum/inventory/` with human and machine indexes, schema, tags, and
log. Any later proposal must therefore pass a reuse/adaptation gate rather than install a competing
knowledge system.

## Smallest executable unit after the gates

A one-source/one-control dry-run:

1. one isolated read-only extractor records an occurrence or explicit absence;
2. one controlled assembler writes only to the confirmed Inventory root;
3. one independent auditor checks source binding, schema, denominator coverage, ambiguity,
   non-authority language, and machine-index parseability.

Native outputs are a source-backed evidence-card or entry, the explicit control result,
`index.md`, `index.json`, tags/log updates, and a validation result. The terminal result is
`PASS | BOUNDED_CORRECTIONS | BLOCK`; it says nothing about whether composition is real, effective,
novel, or correctly defined.

## Preconditions and gates

Before even the dry-run can be proposed as executable:

1. pass the reuse gate by naming a concrete downstream lookup, revalidation, or maintenance need;
2. locate or restore the canonical Inventory source and verify its regeneration path;
3. have its owner ratify a bounded/unregistered bootstrap with roles, topology, immutable source
   and mutation boundaries, confirmation, validation, terminal behavior, and close responsibility;
4. prove that host binding and close can be represented honestly without a fabricated dispatch
   type, ledger row, or route receipt;
5. add and pass positive and authority-escape fixtures;
6. produce and confirm an exact corpus manifest with repository-relative paths and digests;
7. freeze every seat, prompt, budget, output, write target, retry ceiling, and validation command;
8. obtain explicit human confirmation of that complete frozen proposal.

If any item fails, the result remains `BLOCK`; there is no fallback to Research.

## Semantic risks

- treating Inventory as “Research without verdicts”;
- treating descriptive fields as a definition of composition;
- generalizing the lens case to composition in general;
- inferring causal effect from co-occurrence, configuration, sequence, or synthesis;
- hiding absences, ambiguity, contradictions, exclusions, or residue;
- claiming corpus completeness without a frozen denominator;
- editing a generated skill surface as if it were canonical;
- inventing a registered identity or lifecycle because the ledger can represent a similar word;
- installing a second knowledge system instead of adapting the existing Inventory.

## Downstream separation

Once the descriptive inventory is accepted, a separately routed registered Research dispatch may
form and adjudicate candidates about recurrence, neighboring phenomena, mechanisms, or boundaries.
It must consume Inventory records as non-authoritative evidence, apply Research's own verdict
contract, and receive its own confirmation. The two stages must not be collapsed.
