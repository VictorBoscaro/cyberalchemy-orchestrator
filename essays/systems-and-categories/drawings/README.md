# Drawings

The drawings should make relations perceptible without pretending the essay has proved more structure than it has.

For the next revision, keep the same underlying situation across lens panels and vary only what becomes salient. Use solid lines for sustained relations, dashed lines for uncertain or disputed ones, and visible gaps for relations known to be absent. Keep labels at least 9 pt at final size. The current PNGs are inherited sources; future substantive edits should move the relevant figure to an editable vector source.
