# Findings: priority diagrams

## Decision frame

The evidence supports five **diagram interventions**, not five additional illustrations. The essay already has four figure slots. The priority set below combines a recurring anchor, two new conceptual figures, and two substantial redesigns. Its purpose is to reveal the argumentative structure with the smallest coherent visual vocabulary.

## Authorial constraint: references without a procession of names

Victor Boscaro added this direction after the agent synthesis. The prose should carry at most one or two author names. All relevant references must remain present, and each claim must retain a pointer to its supporting source at the appropriate moment, but the sentence itself should normally foreground the problem, distinction, or result rather than the scholar who introduced it.

This is the author's decision, not an agent finding. It applies to future philosophical refinement as well as the existing systems, category-theory, modeling, analogy, and cognition references. It does **not** authorize removing citations, compressing distinct sources into an unsupported generalization, or making intellectual provenance difficult to recover. The expected pattern is concept-first prose with unobtrusive parenthetical or note-level pointers and a complete reference list.

## Ranked top five

### 1. One situation, successive descriptions, then lenses

**Purpose:** Give the essay a persistent concrete object before formal vocabulary appears, then let the reader recognize it when Section 7 names lenses.

**Why relevant:** The sales example in Section 1 already moves through acquisition, retention, and promise/capacity. Section 7 later explains that different relations become salient enough to govern the next question. At present these moments are conceptually linked but visually separate.

**What it says:** A situation need not be replaced for inquiry to change. Different relational selections disclose different plausible questions and actions. The figure does not claim a formal invariant object; its sameness is only the shared episode and labeled elements.

**Drawing:** A quiet common substrate of sales/fulfillment relations appears in three successive fields. Each field accents a different subset while keeping the others faint but recoverable. The final pair recurs in Section 7 as systemic and categorical investigation, with the categorical panel actually introducing representations and a translation question rather than merely recoloring business edges.

**Evidence:** `pdf/systems-and-categories.md:24-33`, `pdf/systems-and-categories.md:117-133`; reports 01 §1, 02 findings 3, 03 findings 1-2. Artbook sources: *A family of graph views* and, with caution, *Invariant identity under projection*.

### 2. Translation as a preservation test

**Purpose:** Replace the present visual suggestion that similar-looking graphs make a translation valid.

**Why relevant:** This is the essay's central bridge from analogy to mathematics. The present Figure 1 shows correspondence but not the obligation that can succeed or fail.

**What it says:** Translation is not resemblance. A declared relation or composed path on one side creates a preservation question on the other; the gate marks an obligation to verify, not a proof already obtained.

**Drawing:** Two deliberately non-identical structured representations. A candidate correspondence crosses between them. On both sides, a two-step path and its composite are visible; a small transverse gate marks where mapping and composition must be compared. Solid, dashed, and interrupted marks distinguish declared correspondence, candidate analogy, and failed or unavailable preservation.

**Evidence:** `pdf/systems-and-categories.md:51-66`; reports 01 §1 item 4, 02 finding 1, 03 sequence item 2. Artbook source: *Witnessed composition*.

### 3. Earned formalization, with legitimate stopping points

**Purpose:** Make the subtitle's movement "toward domain languages" visible without depicting category theory as an inevitable maturity ladder.

**Why relevant:** Section 4 contains the essay's distinctive methodological claim: vocabulary may acquire types, signatures, identities, and composition only when domain consequences warrant them.

**What it says:** Stronger form is earned by obligations encountered in work. A domain language may stop at any adequate level; category-like structure is conditional, not the ordained destination.

**Drawing:** A path begins with ordinary distinctions and reaches typed relations. Later transitions pass through small gates labeled by the domain pressure they must answer: admissible composition, identities, associativity. At every threshold, a quiet valid endpoint branches away, showing that further formalization is optional.

**Evidence:** `pdf/systems-and-categories.md:70-80`; reports 01 §1 item 2 and 02 finding 4. Artbook source: the gate and absent shortcut from *Witnessed composition*.

### 4. Rupture, residue, and revision without erasure

**Purpose:** Prevent the reader from collapsing representational failure into an incorrect data instance or an obvious repair.

**Why relevant:** Section 6 carries a dense but essential distinction: a previously useful language may fail to express a newly consequential difference, and the resulting residue underdetermines its revision.

**What it says:** The prior representation was not necessarily dishonest or useless. A new task exposes a distinction it cannot preserve. Several repairs may follow, and the failure itself does not choose among them.

**Drawing:** One earlier path reaches a witnessed rupture. The earlier continuation remains faint and addressable. A hollow interruption marks what the current language cannot express; two or more dashed candidate revisions depart from it, with none promoted as the answer.

**Evidence:** `pdf/systems-and-categories.md:103-113`; reports 01 §1 item 3, 02 finding 2, 03 local tension 1. Artbook sources: *Revision without erasure* and *Append-only strata*.

### 5. Transversal languages with recoverable context

**Purpose:** Redesign Figure 4 around one job: show bounded work coexisting with languages that participate unevenly across the path and context that remains recoverable.

**Why relevant:** This is the essay's application to ResonantOS and corrects the earlier mistake of placing domain language only inside local work. The current figure still risks implying uninterrupted semantic continuity and tries to carry too many concepts.

**What it says:** Languages may cross intention, orchestration, bounded work, and effect without becoming uniform or universal. Where languages meet, translation may be explicit, uncertain, or unresolved. Recoverability is not the same as continuous presence.

**Drawing:** Four distinct work stages, crossed by overlapping language fields of unequal extent. Small junctions mark translations; offset or open junctions mark unresolved mismatches. Thin retrieval traces connect stages to a recoverable contextual field below. No band encloses the whole and no canonical common axis is asserted.

**Evidence:** `pdf/systems-and-categories.md:137-176`; reports 01 §1 item 5, 02 finding 5, 03 sequence item 5. Artbook sources: *Simultaneous scopes*, *A family of graph views*, and *One relation, variable depth*.

## Shared symbol grammar proposed

- Solid line: a relation currently declared or sustained.
- Quiet line: present but not salient in this view.
- Dashed line: candidate, historical, or unresolved — the caption must name which; one dash style cannot silently mean all three.
- Visible gap or hollow endpoint: relation unavailable or distinction not expressible.
- Field or contour: a representation, scope, or situated view.
- Small transverse gate: an obligation or warrant must be checked; it never means success by itself.

The visual skin of the artbook — handwriting, beige field, rust palette, and full-page note grid — is not part of the transfer.

## Near misses

### Syntax, compositional semantics, empirical adequacy

Important and worth retaining, but current Figure 2 already performs its main argumentative job. It needs a minor non-entailment refinement and alignment with the common symbols, not priority as a new concept.

### Simultaneous scopes as a standalone figure

Highly compatible with lenses and multiple domain languages, but most of its value can be absorbed into the infrastructure redesign. A separate figure would repeat overlap without adding a new argumentative transition.

### One relation, variable depth

Elegant for the user/orchestrator relationship, but it belongs more directly to the work-context essay. Here it would pull attention toward interface architecture and away from representation and domain language.

### Authority as containment / authorized aperture

Useful infrastructure symbols, but authority is not developed enough in this essay to justify a principal figure. Importing them would expand the essay's scope.

### Planes aligned through one record

Visually attractive for translation, but it asserts a common record or stable axis that this essay has not established. Only its idea of correspondence without uniformity may be borrowed.

### Append-only strata

Strong secondary support for revision history, but redundant as a standalone figure once rupture/residue preserves the earlier path.

### Continuity versus recoverability — high impact

- **Essay layer:** omitted, uncertain, and mismatched context should remain recoverable.
- **Current visual layer:** uninterrupted bands imply stable continuity through every stage.
- **Disposition:** replace continuous bands with junctions and retrieval traces if accepted. Human confirmation pending.

- **Reading layer:** recurring geometry helps recognition.
- **Epistemic layer:** a stable axis or central invariant would claim more identity than the essay has earned.
- **Disposition:** repeat labels and episode, not a formal invariant carrier. Human confirmation pending.

### New full figure for formalization versus rupture — medium impact

- **Argument layer:** both are central and presently lack figures.
- **Pacing layer:** giving both full figures crowds Sections 4-7.
- **Disposition:** keep both in the conceptual top five, but decide during layout whether one becomes a smaller unnumbered motif. Human confirmation pending.
