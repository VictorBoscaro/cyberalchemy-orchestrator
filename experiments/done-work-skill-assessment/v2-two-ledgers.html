04 — Evidence boundary

What this report could not see, stated plainly.

Work that leaves no ledger row is invisible to this report's spine. The partition in §1 and §2 is built on 37 dispatch rows. Inline authoring by the parent — no dispatch, no close, no spawn count — produces no row at all, and so cannot appear in either band regardless of how much of the day it consumed or how important its output was.

The day's largest deliverable is an instance of exactly that. The 17-file, +7,657-insertion short print edition includes two complete editions named Signal Spine (version-01) and Recursive Orbit (version-02), each with HTML, PDF and a review log — version-02 with three separate review-round files. Searching the entire ledger for work-context-short-v01, work-context-short-v02, signal-spine, recursive-orbit, Signal Spine or Recursive Orbit returns zero matches, file-wide — not merely in-window. The two editions that shipped have no dispatch row, while the five that failed have ten between them. A state-partitioned report reads the ledger and concludes the short-edition work was a total failure; git shows two finished editions and a reviewed third. Both are true. Only the union is honest.

This report's own structure has a cost, and it is not hidden. Partitioning by state scatters a single objective across two or three bands. The short-edition effort appears in §1 (four editions unfinished), in §3 (Polished Diagrams Plus), and again in this boundary (the two that shipped without rows) — and the why is restated at each appearance instead of being composed once. The backlog skill splits the same way: its dispatch is in §3, its surviving blocker is a host-binary defect described in two different nodes, and its committed output is counted in §2's file totals. A reader wanting the arc of one objective will have to assemble it. That is the price of the ranking key, and it was paid deliberately so that nothing open could ever sort below something finished.

Failure has no text. exit_reason: error carries no message anywhere in this ledger, and user_abort carries no reason. Twelve of the fourteen unfinished items are non-resolved closes, so for twelve of fourteen this report proves that something ended badly and can say nothing at all about what. Every diagnosis in §1 and §3 is therefore drawn from git or from the nodes, never from the failure record itself. The feedback_prompts field is empty for every row in the file and was not used.
